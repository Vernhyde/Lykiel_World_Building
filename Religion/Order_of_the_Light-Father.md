`Acolytes are the name for Mages and Junior Followers`  
`Sages are Experienced Followers`  
`Light-Bearers are usually the Elite Members who lead a group of Acolytes and Sages`

`Vuhaldir’s Religious Symbol is the Corrupted Star.`  
`So even though it has an Ominous Name the Corrupted Star is a symbol of Vuhaldir at his peak. A brilliant white light blasting out as it engulfs a corrupting crimson red of the Great Jasakii Wurm (think of them like Extra Dimensional Aliens).`

`Its a Star that Holds the Corruption within itself, Vuhaldir is also visibly tainted since said event so now his bright pure white form has crimson red eyes`
