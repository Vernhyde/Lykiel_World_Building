Monsters:

Human [ˈ(h)yo͞omən]  
Personality: Various.  
Skin Tone: Various.  
Eye Shape/Color: Various.  
Size: Averages around 5'-6' Feet Tall and about 150-250 Lbs.  
Maximum Age: 110 Y/o  
Special Features: They are good and bad at everything.

Elves [elvz]  
Personality: Cocky, Outgoing and Energetic Types.  
Skin Tone: Ranges from Pale to Different Shades of Pale Mint Green with skin markings ranging from White to Yellow to Gold that glow dimly in the dark.  
Eye Shape/Color: Human Eyes but typically in brighter colors of Red, Blue, Green, Silver, Etc.  
Size: Averages at around 4’5'-5’ Feet Tall and about 100-180 Lbs.  
Maximum Age: 80 Y/o  
Special Features: Sensitive Hearing and Vision, making it easier to hear and see things but also makes them vulnerable to bright unexpected flashes and loud sudden sounds.

Xarten [zärtən]  
Personality: Prideful, Quiet, and Dominant Types.  
Skin Tone: Ranges from Dark Red, Black, Gray, Brown, and Dark Blue. They also have horns ranging in shapes, sizes, and colors. They have been known to grow horns based on power 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. How this is determined is unknown, but this method of telling a Xarten's strength never lies; they will only grow if said Xarten Trains Hard Physically, mentally, oromagically  
Eye Shape/Color: Xarten may have two different types of pupils; Circle Pupils (Humans), and Thick Vertical Slit Pupils (Cat). The eye colors of Xarten are more bright, sometimes glowing and unique ranging from Bright Red, Blue, Green, Gold, Silver, Orange, Yellow, Etc.  
Size: Averages at around 6'-7'6'' Feet Tall and 180-250 Lbs.  
Maximum Age: 300 Y/o  
Special Features: Xarten are typically gifted with Magic and is a Race Known for their Ice, Force, and Sound Magic.

Scythia [sĭthēŭ]  
Personality: Feral, Instinctive, and Rowdy. (A very animal-driven race ,the Scythians are about 60% Feral, 40% Civilized. Civilized Scythians either try to teach Ferals or enslave them. Ferals are either out in the wild or slaves and/or tamed pets to other Scythians and other races.)  
Skin Tone: Various, They are a race known for their Animal Heritage, which gives them qualities and features of their ancestors. 40% of Scythians have the Animal Claws and Feet of their ancestors. (Retractable Claws for Tigers, Non-Retractable Claws for Wolves, Furry Hands and Hooves for Bulls, Etc.) Though on rare occasions some may not have this quality, all Scythians will have a set of ears and a tail from their ancestor. 60% of Scythians have a chance to be Beasts, Fully Fur Covered Scythians. It's rare that they have any sort of average human intelligence, but they do have an adept sense of Friend and Foe, even when lacking intelligence.  
Eye Shape/Color: Various. (That of Ancestors)  
Size: Various. (That of Ancestors).  
Maximum Age: 60-200 Y/o (Depending on Ancestor Animals)  
Special Features: They get the features of their ancestors. Goats are adept at climbing and have iron stomachs. Bulls are thick-headed and sturdy. Tigers are Quick and Quiet. Scythians also have a chance to be born natural Blood Mages though most Ferals do not know how to use it adeptly and usually are very explosive.
