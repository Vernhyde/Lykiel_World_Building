# Delta Company, 1st Battalion, 1st Guard Regiment ("The Old Guard"), 1st Brigade Combat Team, 1st Guard Division, I Corps ("The Keystone"), The Shieldhold Army

## D/1-1 CGR, 1st BCT, 1st GD, I CORPS, SHIELDHOLD ARMY

The Company did what was needed to keep the breakthrough at the Southern Sector contained. Pulled from their detail within the City. They were the closest and quickest to respond to the priority threat. The 5th Division of the Southern Sector was ill prepared for the sudden strike. They were down a handful of cannon batteries for scheduled maintenance, units out on training, there was only one Brigade at full strength in the sector at the time of the attack.

The Monsters slammed the defenses just at dusk, a Battalion was lost within the first 10 minutes at the base of the wall. The Security Forces were unable to assist with this scale of attack due to their lack of military equipment for the larger creatures trying to punch through the wall. They were able to assist with containing any of the little ones that had slipped through the cracks.

The Southern Gateway was the hardest hit. The gargantuan gate-tunnel of old has been industrialized with highways in and out of the city, the Gate taller than nearby buildings themselves meaning it was a very large hole to seal in an attack. The watch Towers and years of retrofitted defenses held the Gate like a border crossing but the years of making it easy to come and go for everyday life meant it lacked the true security of a castle. 

When Delta Company arrived at the Gateway it had to push back a rush coming up north along the highway after those beasts had killed most of the Soldier's stationed there. Aside from support firing down from the much fewer interior wall bunkers. Delta had to hold the first overpass inside the city overlooking the roads through the gate which passed beneath. The Gateway luckily was quite the open tunnel when moving through it. 

While stationed up on that overpass, Delta Company used the most out of the 2 Jackals and 4 Gun Trucks it had at its disposal, holding their position for half an hour of constant fire. Going cyclic to the point of melting one of the truck's 13mm guns. The Cyclone auto cannons of the Jackals going nearly black on ammo having to resort to burning through most of their AP shells after burning through all of their HE. 

Delta lost 102 men that day holding the Gateway Exit till the gate blocks could be blown to drop the gate slamming the Southern tunnel into the city shut. Many lost were in blocking positions below the Overpass. The 5th Division was able to fend off what they could on the outer wall but the lightly manned Gateway likely would have completely been breached if it was not for Delta stopping the tide before they ravaged the inner city and killed thousands.

The remaining 86 soldiers of Delta Company were honored each with The Sovereign Star. The most ever given in a single event in the history of Lumeria alongside numerous other awards.

The Southern Gateway has yet to be unsealed and the Gate lifted, the amount of work needing to be done to do so is enormous alongside repairing the infrastructure surrounding the gate.
