# Awakening

    The damp and musty crypt coughed a smog and dust-filled air as something inside began to shuffle and move. Pushing up the stale air inside as it flowed out through the few openings that remained inside that crypt. Something does not rest still, their peace not yet given to them, as slowly the dead get up. A young corpse, lying on a stone slab shelf, rolled as he gasped awake and groaned out. Eyes darting about as he looks around his location, seemingly confused and lost, coughing further as he struggles to *breathe* the air around him. Though breathing was not really required as the air he sucks through his mouth blows out the hole in his chest, a cleaned wound, a puncture through his lungs, clean yet bare to the exposed elements of the crypts. Seeing this, the young corpse begins to panic, gasping harder and sucking dusty air as he tries to speak and cry out, but his collapsed lungs make it hard to speak, not to mention his stiff vocal cords resisting any movement.  
    In the depths of his panic attack, a voice echoes out in the crypt.  
    "Calm... Take a moment to think...You cannot breathe; do not try to force it, you do not need the air." The voice speaks to the corpse before he looks up with one still functional eye. A large figure clad in grey robes looks down at him. A large woman with deep blue skin, a set of 4 symmetrical horns across her head, adorned with short black hair.  
    "I am Reaper Sesua. I come to help you with your awakening undead..." The female spoke to the corpse softly but in a firm, teaching tone. She was informing him of his situation and who she was, and soon why he was here... why he was... *alive*.  
    "Yes... You had passed away and now have chosen to be awakened to serve a purpose, should you choose to lift yourself from your rest. Otherwise, you can roll over, shut your eyes, and rest once more." She said coldly, giving the corpse a choice to live a new life or be put to rest.  
    The corpse would watch this so-called Reaper for a moment; he recognized the blue woman as a Xarten, though something about her felt alien and calming. His panic died down as he watched her with his one eye before then reaching out to grab her hand, accepting her offer as the corpse pulled himself from his shelf with her help. The Reaper was supporting him and holding him as his legs struggled to function. Needing to learn to walk once again as they had grown stiff, but her touch and presence seemed to have given him more life and vitality. The two soon walked arm in arm out of the depths of the Crypt, pushing aside the dust and smog as the Reaper and her Undead walked into the cool, fresh night of the world above the resting grounds of the dead.
