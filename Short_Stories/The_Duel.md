# The Duel    

    The concussive roar of rune machinery tore across the muddy and pit marked terrain as the skies lit up with an array of colors from the batteries protecting the skies above their respective lines of man and machine. Large thuds shaking the ground as Knights maneuvered to clear the trenches and craters crossing across the torn lands.  
    The massive Vanguard Knights holding their shields in a low ready as they crossed into no-mans-land. They moved in a quick and smooth manner as the noise would quickly give away their attack as they tried to blitz their way across the lines. The crunch of splintered wood and bone smashed into pulverized mush beneath the large metal feet of these knights as they stayed on high alert.  
    Their attack would be met with a response as the other line was no meer pushover and would quickly be alerted by the thudding and imposing size of these rune machines. Bellowing alarms sounding all up and down the lines as the defenders arming their spell throws began to open up the cacophony of destruction and beautiful color as the cycle and pattern of the 'munitions belts slammed an array of spells into the shield of the knights as those slabs of metal crackled and brushed off the fire and ice that slammed into them.  
    The roar of battle began to fill the area as a heavy weapons knight pulled around the right side of a Vanguard his auto cannon spinning to life as he rips into the trenches and small men that manned it. Blasts of electricity and acid filling the holes in the mud as the screams of victims would barely be heard over the roar of guns and cannons.  
    This devastating burst would be interrupted by sudden blast of energy as a beam of crackling blue light ripped through the air piercing the auto cannon and ripping it out of the hands of the knight. The blast throwing it to the ground as the knight collided with the earth and mud massive hands clawing to make his way back to his feet.  
    “ENEMY KNIGHTS IMBOUND." A female voice blasted from one of the Vanguards as she stomped between the direction of fire and the fallen Assault Knight. Bracing her shield and attempting to cover the fallen knight as they reorientate their heavy body.  
    The attack would rip through the air again smashing into the shield of the vanguard protecting her brother-knight. As across the burning and melting trenches of man came roaring 2 opposing Vanguard-Class Knights as the crackling energy blast came from a Hunter-Class Knight sitting far in the back with a massive Knight Slayer Cannon.  
    Another Friendly Vanguard would roar into motion as the large hunk of metal began to charge to meet the counter attack with an interception as the Assault Knight got to his feet. As soon as he was up the female Vanguard was quick into maneuvering to help her partner Vanguard duel these incoming enemies. Drawing a large rune blade from it's sheath as the 4 massive knight clashed with blade and shield. Their massive frames making deafening noises as they clashed and slammed into each other. Blade and shield clashing and slashing at each other.  
    The Assault knight now without his auto cannon needed to maneuver to challenge this Hunter or at least pull his attention away from the Vanguards as they dueled to keep his comrade from being targeted next.  
    "LIGHTEN LOAD TO INCREASE MOBILITY." A male voice came from the Assault as he seemed to talk to himself a tactical recommendation as the Knight moved to strip the heavy container of ammunition for the Auto cannon throwing it to the ground now significantly lighter than before as he began to move. Lifting his feet which much more resolve and vigor. The knight pulling a large cylinder from his side as he skipped a step, preparing to launch this cylinder as the massive arms of the knight launched it like a trebuchet as soon as he released the container into the air it began to form runes and magic rings as if it was charging up a spell.  
    As he launched it the Hunter took notice aiming to try and intercept the container but quickly realizing he would not beat it, quickly dashing to the left away from the container as it suddenly bounced off of his former position before suddenly ripping mud, blood, metal and wood into the now black sphere engulfing the container as it imploded in a massive sucking explosion.  
    The Hunter attempted to restabilize his massive rifle to bring it to bear on the quickly closing Assault as it stomped faster and faster towards the Hunter blade drawn and ready as the Hunter had to let off a rushed shot or his rifle would soon be useless. The crackling blue energy ripping through the air again.  
    "AUTO MANUVERING." The Knight seemed to warn again as it suddenly ducked and weaved to the side in a much more fluid manner than it's previous movements. The energy scrapping along it's pauldron as the Knight suddenly smashed his shoulder into the Hunter easily knocking the heavy rifle from his grasp as a blade drove deep into the chest of the knight just barely missing the center.  
    "EJECT, EJECT!" The Hunter roared before suddenly his back plates were thrown from his body as a much smaller humanoid Pilot was thrown from his chassis yelling something.  
    The Assault Knight wanted to kill this pilot but the threat was neutralized for now and they still had 2 enemy knights in the area, he needed to help his comrades. Pulling his blade from the Hunter's chest as he quickly turned to charge back into battle.  
    It was not looking good either, the female Vanguard was now fighting both enemy Vanguards as her partner laid face down in the muck his back still sealed. Her sword clashing and parrying as best it could as she fought defensively and tried to fend off the counter attack. The Assault would come to her rescue though as one of the vanguards suddenly relented as it's head spun on it's shoulders to see the Assault Knight approaching with his dagger drawn. The large Vanguard now pivoting his whole body as he bellowed.  
    "ADJUSTING TARGET." The Knight prepared his blade to duel the much faster Assault Knight as the now smaller Knight tried to go in for a quick kill. Bracing his dagger at his side for a lunge as the Vanguard met him with a down slash and braced shield. The Assault Knight feinting his blade to upswing against the falling blade, stomping his foot into the ground to suddenly stop his movement as his backup dagger turned to slice through the falling wrist of the Vanguard, separating his sword from his body.  
    The Vanguard did not react to the loss of his blade and instead rewarded the Assault with a kiss of his shield as he shield bash the nimble knight to the ground before raising his shield to punch the assaulter with the hard edge of the shield. Smashing it into his head and body over and over.  
    "INCOM-" The head of the vanguard spun too late as a blade drove through the center of his back piercing his chest as a relatively small amount of blood dripped from the large sword clearly hitting the pilot as the Vanguard went lifeless and limp.  
    The Female Vanguard had won her duel and came to help the Assault with his as she pulled her blade from the enemy's back letting the large hunk of metal crash to the ground as he helped her dented comrade to his feet before handing him an opponent's sword.  
    "YOU OWE ME." The Knight bellowed before they stepped over to check on their comrade and check the status of the Knight and Pilot.
