# `Plate of Ceramic and Rune`

`Shrapnel bounced off the old concrete, flying into the dirt-covered tile floor of the building as a rocket crashed into the 5 floor of a contested apartment complex. The chunks of concrete were raining down onto the cracked street and dirt below. Rounds quickly following as the explosion sucked in the air from nearby, shells shattering against the building and breaking what little glass remained before a volley returned from the complex. Soldiers battle for what little worth those above them place on their lives. Any grain of sand across the frontline was progress as the city roared with fighting.`   
`The sky was aglow with the glitter of crystalline artillery as shells rained down onto the city. Ripping blocks apart with toxic fumes, hellfire, and a mixture of every bad thing man could know. Drakes posted high on buildings, raining shells down from the cannons mounted to their powerful backs, the wingless dragons hammering any position they and their team can see. Their cousins soared fast and low above the buildings as they moved as fast as their wings could take them, dumping high-drag bombs beneath them as the wing beats of Dragons usually prefaced the pounding thuds of bomblets hammering the land.`   
`Though the unlucky Flights of Dragons would be intercepted if they got too close to heavily controlled enemy defensive positions. The Typical response being a sonic crack of a Wyvern and its missiles as it intercepts any prey in the air. Though Mages positioned in vantage points or beasts of steel poised on the streets below could bring down the death-bringers from above.`   
`All of this was reported by man, beast, and drone above as those who planned the war had plans of their design on how it should be finished. Though the Victor was determined by those on the ground and in the air, just as much as those back home.`
