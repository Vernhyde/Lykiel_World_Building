# No Knock

    The soft creaks of wooden chairs and floorboards, the flickering of soft light glowing from crystal lanterns, gems floating inside a glass case as they give their light to the room around them. Shadows cast long against the walls and furniture, rifles sitting upon a table with magazines and loose ammunition next to them. Preparations for an upcoming fight, and it seemed to be a big one as well.  
    Although that fight would not come, the men preparing for war would be caught completely off guard, sleeping in their beds and drinking away their anxiety. The door to the sub-level apartment slowly pulled open to the darkened hall outside, its silence hiding the force well prepared for *this* fight. A stack of bodies pushing into the room, under the cover of silence. The noise in the area was completely cut off from the world as the vibrations in the air were still.  
    The first would be the man sitting on the couch, two bright flashes in this artificial silence shattered his glass as he held it and embedded two rounds into his chest as he fell back in his seat, going limp as a third blew the back of his skull from his head. The silent assault would continue into the dining room where the arms and weapons were lying about, a man coming to realize he could not hear a thing, and noticing the flashes from the next room over, went to arm himself, picking up a rifle and loading it just as the point man came into the room, silent fire being exchanged in quick succession, the first man taking a round to his lower plate knocking him a little back and staggering his stance as a comrade came to support, leveling his rifle next to him and aiming to the fighter and dropping him in quick order.  
    The rest of the team had moved past this initial fight, down a hall to clear the rest of the rooms. Each man knew his place and direction; each man was trained to work in this unnatural silence, as the fighters were quickly put down in their sleep, far from their homeland. Few were lucky enough to even witness their last moments; soon, all will be laid to rest before the silence is lifted. The same creaking from before is now being heard due to the heavy boots on the floor, the dust of the raid settling down now as the raiders finally speak.  
    "Serpent 1 to Atlas, Site 3 is clear. We have one injury, minor. Performing SSE." One of the men reported over the radio before the team began to sift through the apartment for anything this Cell may have. The man who had been previously struck with a round had a figure cloaked in white standing next to him, a white furred claw extending down to the man as he sat on the arm of a couch, leaning back, letting the claw hover over his abdomen. A soft glow came from it as the figure monitored the man.  
    "Broken Ribs... 2... Bruising..." Growled out a voice from under the white hood.  
    "You'll see a healer after this..." The figure added before moving away from the man, walking over to a corpse on the couch, before reaching his claw out once more. The glow came stronger this time as the body on the couch contorted a little before the corpse gasped for air, letting out rattling, raspy breaths as it began to speak. Seemingly not aware of its state as it recalled thoughts like lost memories, needing to be reminded to oneself out loud.  
    "I... hope.. Fario's right... the Jasakii promised us...... bombs placed.... few days... train station... Why is it... quiet?" The corpse recalled as one of the soldiers approached the white figure and spoke up.  
    "I'll call it in... fuckin cultists." He stated with disgust as the others watched the corpse fall limp again.  
    "They look to sow chaos in the city... remember... their promises are false... they wish to see you all dead... do not listen to their lies... follow the light of the father..." The figure said in his deep growling tone as he turned to the one who had spoken to him.
