`It rained, it had been raining heavily for the past couple days, the earth was thoroughly muddy and saturated due to the flooding of the valley. It was a pain to travel through and for an unlucky crew that was just what they were doing. A Lumerian Army Unit had been sent out due to an alert that had been triggered at an automated sensor detection array, it was likely a regular faulty trigger and they would be driving back to FOB Blackwood soon enough, but for the frontier units this was something they had to do, just in case something did actually happen. The current unit that was responding to this call was 1st Squad of II Platoon, Charlie Company, 3rd Wolfen Infantry. They were out on Patrol in the area already earlier that day and had diverted their route to travel to the Array only extending their patrol in theory to a couple more hours. The 15 man unit had a little bit of boost to their numbers with the addition of a pair of quadrupedal hauler constructs. The Donkey sized constructs assist the men with the hauling of their packs and launchers to help lighten their own loads. The two machines diligently carried the gear with no issue as the unit walked through the still damp and muddy Valley. The thunk of boots and casual small talk expressing just how relaxed the soldiers were as they kicked aside stones and debris. The heavy rain had brought down an assortment of tree branches and even cracked a few trees apart as they had given out to the strong winds.`  
`Staff Sargent Erik Johnson was the man leading this patrol, taking his team deeper into the Valley, in the distance they could see the concrete small concrete bunker sitting on top of one of the ridges with a clean view out to the Wolfen Border a few kilometers away west of here. The structure had been repurposed a few years back as it used to be an observation post with actual men manning the concrete block but now with the advent of the technology it sits with a large sensor mast and the bunker just keeps the rain off the equipment inside. The conflict farther south had not come this far north as the war had been dragging to a slog, that was why they were not really concerned about the issues the mast had and just sent 1st Squad out here to come investigate the issue so that they knew what the engineers needed to bring out here without coming themselves first.`  
`“Hey Sarge, the mast seems to be in one piece so at least the storm didn’t blow it over.” A young elf called back to Johnson from the lead position, a new rifleman, Peter Horten.`  
`“Yeah I can see that, it’s likely a leak or some torn wires that fried the shit.” Johnson replied back to the private as he walked along behind his first fireteam, the other two teams of his unit following behind him, keeping pace but also keeping their space. They had enough men that the squad could trail quite the distance while still being within reach of a comrade. The team would make their way up the valley ridge, carefully watching their steps to make sure that they did not launch a rock down the slope at their brothers or worse yet trigger a mud slide. The constructs were trailing close behind the squad trying to get their robotic footing in the mud carefully as they worked their way up the slope with them, luckily they were behind the unit because if one of those constructs take a tumble and hit someone it was going to break a lot of bones being quite the heavy beasts of steel they were.`  
`As Peter neared concrete structure he would look back at the rest of the unit behind him before then looking ahead to wet entrance of the concrete block house, leveling his rifle off in front of him as he stepped inside first to take a peek inside, the rest of Alpha team following him inside along with the Staff Sargent, the other two teams being left outside to pull security while they investigated.`   
`“Damnnit!” Peter blurted suddenly as his foot suddenly sank into a hole in the concrete floor, dunking his boot completely in that flooded hole before he pulled it out and shook that waterlogged boot as best he could before continuing on.`  
`“That’s why you go first Horten, now my boot will stay dry!” The man behind Peter joked and teased him as he avoided the flooded hole, the men walking along this flooded hallway into the old observation bunker, It was eerily quiet, likely due to the machines inside being dead as the team filed into the main room of the bunker with old clouded windows looking out into the horizon from the ridge. Old chairs and newer computer equipment were packed into this room as the 5 men looked around the place quietly.`   
`Johnson noticed it first, the computers looked like they had been water boarded but there was not a clear way for them to get wet. There wasn’t a crack in the ceiling or a hole in the wall spraying water, in fact it seemed like ONLY the equipment got wet. There also was a suspiciously clean smear across one of the screens, as if someone had wiped the dust and grime away to see.`   
`“Eyes up, I don’t think we were the only ones here.” Johnson said calmly into his PTT letting the two teams outside know to shut up and keep their eyes peeled as the air grew tense. The men in the bunker took things a bit slower now as they were more attentive, knowing they may have something on their hands now.`  
`Peter stepped his soggy boot over to a dark doorway inside the bunker, a side room, likely a storage closet or something similar as he grabbed a flashlight from a pouch on his side and shined it into the darkness. The Elf sees a weird carving in the wall across from him and decides to step closer to see better before suddenly the whole wall lit up with a shocking blue glow in a flash.`   
`Johnson saw that and on instinct grabbed the short rifleman by his collar, yanking him hard to the side. “COVER!” He yelled as he did so and men dove out of the way of the doorway as fast as their feet could take them. A bright blue plasma suddenly blasted through the room, slamming into the wall opposite of the sigil as it melted the concrete with a hot bright jet of heat. The trap was here to kill anyone who investigated the array clearly.`  
	`“OUT! OUT! EVERYONE OUT NOW!” Erik yelled to Alpha team as the men quickly bailed on the room, Erik releasing Peter so that the two of them could follow the rest of Alpha team back to the bright outside, they were not gonna risk running into more Script Traps in there. Johnson got onto the radio and called back his command to let them know it was sabotage and trapped, and that so far every man was accounted for.`   
`Though as soon as Johnson got exterior he would hear the commotion as a couple men gathered around one on the ground, trying to assist with tending to his wound, he had been outside the wall that had just been jet blasted and it took a smoldering bolt of plasma to his waist, searing through the side of his stomach and flesh.`  
`“Blackwood Main, this is Walker 3-1, we have 1 injured, prepare to copy MIST report, over” Johnson called back to the FOB as he overlooked the ongoing wrestling match as the soldiers tried to keep their brother from moving too much as he screamed in pain. Johnson’s Assistant Lead worked to keep the other men focused on security, worried about potentially more than just a trap now as they could be in the eyes of an ambush.`  
`“Walker 3-1, This is Blackwood Main. Prepared to Copy, Over.” Johnson’s radio blared to life after a moment, now with new urgency in the voice.`   
`"Blackwood Main, Walker 3-1. Mechanism: Booby trap Script. Injury: Molten Jet Blast, appears in and out through lower left abdomen. Signs: Conscious, pulse elevated, bleeding controlled. Treatment: Pressure dressing secured. Requesting 9-Line evacuation, over." Johnson replied as he knelt down next to the soldiers and the one who was working on keeping the Injured’s bandage pressured overlooking the injured as he spoke.`  
`“Blackwood Main copies MIST. Send your 9-Line, over.” The radio chattered back after a moment.`  
`“Line One: Grid Echo-Uniform 1-4-2-7-5-2. Line Two: 3-7 Point 5-0, Walker 3-1. Line Three: One Alpha. Line Four: Bravo. Line Five: One Lima. Line Six: Papa. Line Seven: Bravo. Line Eight: Alpha. Line Nine: None. Over.” Johnson calmly replied into the radio letting his training take the reigns as he speaks with a clearer voice than he ever had.`   
`“Blackwood Main copies all Nine Lines. Medevac is spinning up, ETA fifteen Minutes. Monitor this Frequency. Out.”`  
`“Okay, keep him here, we got 15 minutes.” Johnson told the CLS as he worked to keep the injured soldier down and warm. Johnson stood back up now to see that his ASL had pushed Charlie and Alpha to the South and North Respectively, overlooking the opposite sides of the ridge while looking down into the Western Valley. Bravo had been kept here to stay with their brother and pull close security for them. The teams waiting an excruciating 13 minutes before Johnson’s radio chattered back to life.`   
`"Walker Three-One, this is Dustoff Four-Five. Inbound your station, two minutes out. Mark your zone, over."`

Johnson replied as he moved a few steps away from the others, underhanding a green smoke grenade out into the dirt in front of him, watching as it pops and fizzles before it began to slowly billow out smoke, soon creating a steady stream and pillar as the beating of propellers came louder into the distances as soon that flying metal machine, came into view`
