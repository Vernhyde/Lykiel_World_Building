# The Meeting  

    "Silence." Growled out a voice as the man receiving the order stood there in the middle of the office, his hands neatly but anxiously folded in front of him as he spoke to the ***thing*** sitting behind the desk. The white Scythian wolf avoiding it's gaze as he looked down at the ancient wooden desk.  
    "I do not care what Vuhaldir's directive was. He damn well knows that this is **my** territory and he should just let me handle it, keep the fucking Order out of it." The beast behind the desk growled out again as he stood up now, his large, monstrous frame much more visible as he towered over the smaller male. He stood back a little from the desk, but even that distance did not prevent the wolf from looking up at the creature. The large beast stood looking down at the wolf with the infamously pictured skull and his bright yellow, fiery eyes.  
    "I am... I am just the messenger, sir... I was only told to bring you the Grand Envoy's request..." The wolf felt so small, like a pup again. He was just trying not to be killed, as the air was so heavy here, and it felt very much like this Great Beast could kill him, and nothing would happen but be a footnote in the paper.  
    "...Just go, I will deal with this myself..." The beast growled out again, dismissing the man who would quickly scurry out of the office with his tale between his legs. The beast was moving now from behind his desk as he quietly pushed the chair on the other side out of the way before reaching into his heavy blazer to pull something out from the inside. The beast pulls out a vial of pure white fluid as he pops the lid off before pouring the pure white liquid onto the floor, as it spreads with its glowing nature all over the concrete.  
    "⩝𝈳⊦ᒥ⊬ᔪ⊥ᐱ." The beast mumbled in an arcane tongue before he then felt the room flash with a glowing bright light, and the warmth of the Light-Father filled the office space, glowing with a bright pure light.  
    "Elliot... You called me." Standing in the middle of the room was a figure of glowing bright white light, his form hard to see and shape poorly defined, but it was clearly Scythian-like in nature. When he turned his head, the muzzle of a dog could be seen, as the color of the office around him contrasted with his white light to give him shape.  
    "Vuhaldir, you know why. Your damn Order is being a fucking pain in the ass. Your *Grand Assistant* is intruding on my work and business affairs." The beast raised his voice now to the clearly unnatural figure that stood there.  
    "Like...?" The much older white figure retorts to the beast, unable to help him if he does not clearly state his issues.  
    "Where to start? They keep intruding on Vashnalli's fucking assignments, so now my men can't fucking hunt the Jasakii, let alone capture them cause your fanatics kill them on sight even when they are contained. The Zealots also keep trying to intrude on Leechwood's Sites and Factories, so I have to assign more security just to remind them that no, the Order does not have the right to walk wherever the fuck they want and investigate my people for *heresies*. I do not mind helping the Order when they cooperate, but if they are just going to be a nuisance, then I won't stand for it, Vuhaldir." Elliot stated his case firmly and sternly; even if the being in front of him was a god, he still would keep his same old attitude.  
    "To be fair, Elliot... if I recall, they have found a number of Jasakii mimics in those factories... buuut that is besides the point..." The white figure retorted again before he would take a step towards the beast.  
    "I will talk to the Grand Envoy... but I expect you to tighten your own management, you and I both know what is at stake, and the mortals cannot handle this all on their own, they do not have the foresight or length of lives to handle the future." The figure continued to speak as he crossed his arms in front of him.  
    "If I do, then I expect them to help Vashnalli on high-risk assignments... If you get their egos checked, then I can keep sending advisors and trainers, but do know that if they keep fucking it up, they will have to figure out their training and arms procurement on their own." The beast negotiated with God as he stood there and crossed his arms across his chest.  
    "I know well that they have no real good way to handle Leviathans or Titans on their own; they need the Knights." He added on, knowing that the Order, while having many men and mages at their disposal, had no real heavy arms like Vashnalli. The private military is leagues ahead of most of the Order's men in terms of equipment, except for a few sub-orders that have better funding and equipment. Vashnalli could go toe to toe with some nations' armies. The only thing that the Order had here was Political power in Lumeria, as it was such a strong religious front with many worshippers and followers in all levels of society, and even some being in Vashnalli itself.  
    "...Fine.. I will talk to the Grand Envoy and have some changes made, keep helping them... if they make mistakes, then let them, have them learn the hard way if they have to... Do not forsake them, though... they only wish to make Lykiel a better place and rid those pests from our plane... I cannot be everywhere at once. I have to keep this reality together and in one piece. Those things are gnawing at the seams constantly, and the dwindling gods are making it so that only really I can go out and put things back together... Those we have left are not much of fighters... We need to nurture the mortals to manage themselves, else they will be doomed when we are all gone..." Vuhaldir would reply before he relaxed his stance and turned to look off into the distance, but it was as if he was looking at something through the wall, far off into the unknown.  
    "I must go... do remember what I said..." He turned to leave, now taking a step before glancing back.  
    "Oh... also keep your Sister in line... She is causing trouble for the *Ladies* and Juona can only bless her and them so much..." The god chuckled before he took another step and flashed from existence.
