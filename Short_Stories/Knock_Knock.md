# `Knock, Knock`

`The bone crunching shattering of a man's knee was audible even through the deafening blasts of shotgun shells. A handful of copper blowing through his knee before another crashed through his throat, ripping flesh from his throat as it bores a hole through him. The sound of his chords soaking in his blood, gurgling out any remaining air he had left was the last sounds he made. The next man would suffer a similarly fatal fate as another shooter spent a handful of rounds to blow ventilation through his lungs. The lead team putting work in to down any shooters and keeping the momentum for the rest that followed as the 4 man team pushed down another hall further into the complex. When they reached a set of double doors at the end of the hall the point man would touch the handles confirming that it was locked.`   
`Another was moving up as soon as they saw that this door was stopping their momentum. The woman reaching a hand out as she crossed to the other side, palm glowing with a unnatural heat before she swung it towards the door, which as soon as it became parallel to the surface suddenly glowed increasingly bright for a moment. Suddenly the door splintering as the hand blew a small hole through the door. Swinging it hard against the barricade on the other side though this attempt did not succeed in making it traversable unlike the others before. The mage seeing this reached back to grab a special tool for a situation like this. Pulling out a large sledge hammer as the other 3 pulled back a little from the other side of the door to give her some distance as they hold security. The woman handling the hammer as if it was a paperweight, prepping a single swing before using all her might to swing the breaching tool hard. The surface of the steel head suddenly lighting up with carved runes along the edges of the head. The steel hammer suddenly smashing into the door only for the surface of it a split second later bending into a concave shape before blowing the door and the barricade behind it violently into the room. Wind violently rushing through the hall next to the team as it now blew into the now open room. The point man immediately retaking the lead as it pushed through the breach just to be followed by the rest of the team continuing the violent and forceful raid deeper into the complex.`  
	`The chatter of rotary blades hummed through the air as they chopped their way through it, carrying upon it the weight of a steel beast. The machine armed with various deadly implements of war, singing their way across the sky vaporizing anything it can see as the machine drew it's autocannon up to fire down upon man and beast below. Tearing into the buildings, ripping flesh from bone as the deafening roar of the cannon was followed with another howling roar as a missile tears away from it's pod, slamming into another structure, blowing a hole into the roof as smoke billowed out of every opening it could. It was wreaking havoc to those below with no immediate response at their disposal just hoping to have the luck to avoid the gaze of the flying death machine.`   
`The pause in the fire revealed the high pitch whistling sound of another in the skies above as suddenly the steel beast rippled two more missiles from the ends of it's pylons, tearing off into the distant sky as the gunship began to pull back from the battlefield, it's gun spinning to life as it aimed to the horizon, chattering away as suddenly with a loud snap and woosh the gunship barely dodge a flying projectile. It's quick maneuver throwing into a unbalanced path as it tried to strafe as fast as it could the whistling turning into a roar before suddenly a large winged beast ripped across the sky just barely missing and passing the gunship which tried to turn around to fight this newcomer.`   
`The large wyvern had come around for another strike, quickly diving on the gunship again as neck mounted pods began to spit out bright and hot decoys as the beast blasted the helicopter with it's breath weapon. Smashing straight into the cockpit, immediately disabling the threat as the craft began to spin now out of control before bursting into a fireball as soon as it impacted the streets and building below. It really stood little chance with it's primarily ground attack loadout, fighting off a Wyvern Interceptor was going to be quite difficult even for the best pilots.`
