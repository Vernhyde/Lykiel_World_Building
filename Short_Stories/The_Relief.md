    "Wake up-" A swift nudge and a cold voice woke the woman as she shuffled and sat up quickly from her rest. Startled a little bit due to the edge she was already on. Her uniform was a little wrinkled from her slouched sleeping as she moved to get up looking at the one that woke her. Her gaze meets the large analyzing singular optic lens of the Golem that looked down at her. It was her Knight and she was their Pilot. The Knight eyed her for a moment as his optic whirled and whizzed as it focused and adjusted while watching her. It was a moment of silence but one that was appreciated as it gave her a fleeting moment to calm herself a little. The Knight's relatively small arrowhead shaped head was covered in lens and sensors. Giving it an ever so slightly more humanoid appearance as the sleek arrowhead flicked off to the side to look elsewhere.  
    "My Halo Array is picking up unidentified contacts on passive..." The Knight warned their pilot quietly as the large machine sat nearly hunched over her. It was shielding her with it's mass in their little hiding spot that they had been using to rest.  
    "They are lacking IFF designations. Though they are large contacts, it appears that they are at least Two other Knights though I cannot confirm without sending out a wave." The Knight states further on the situation they have found themselves in. In their low activity state and hiding within this older run down barn the Knight and their pilot have gone undetected so far.  
    Upon hearing this the woman would spring up and move to be off to the side as the lower energy golem released it's hatch to let her climb into the centrally located space in the Golem's artificial body. The pilot hooking her self into her partner as they quietly listen to the signals together while they waited in their hidden state. 200.. 150.. 100 Meters, slowly the pair of signatures continued to walk before one stopped it's movement soon followed by the other.  
    "Brace-" The Knight echoed as soon as she spoke it as suddenly the Halo Array echoed out across the spectrum, throwing a wave from their position to detect the exact makeup of those two contacts. The machine heaving up just as it blows through the roof of the small barn it had crawled so carefully into. It was prepared to fight the unidentified foe just as the Halo's ping came back. Rotary Cannon spinning to life as rounds began to funnel down it's belt.  
    As the gun was aimed it suddenly pulled upwards as the information flooded into the system and the units had properly been identified. The knight trying to avoid ripping 30mm shells into allies as the two knights 100 meters away had picked up them when they drew near and had raised their own weapons in anticipation. The data luckily flowed fast enough through both sides that they identified each other before anyone fired.  
    "Ally- 4th Knight Brigade, 2nd Battalion, 1st Company, 2nd Platoon.." The Golem listed off their identification as it's gun spun back down, the clutch that loaded it's rounds having not engaged keeping the barrels empty. The duo slowly walking across that wet field to approach the other two knights who had lowered their own weapons. One model seemed to be a bit bigger than the other though the mean looking cannon that the smaller one held looked like a good reason to never overlook him due to his size. The larger mech offering his left hand to shake the middle sized knight as it approached. The left handed shake common among golems due to the fitment of semi permanent tools and weapons attached to their right limb.  
    This shake allowing the two to sync their systems as communications came to life within the cockpit as the knight turned to shake the smaller one next.  
    "Greetings\!\!" Announced a older sounding man, cheerful though with quite the accent to his voice.  
    "We were not expecting any company out here." The old man chuckled as he watched the loner.  
    "Nor were we, caught us with our pants down a bit..." The machine responded back before it's pilot did.  
    "Are you guys out here on patrol?" The woman finally had a chance to speak and ask her question getting a quick response back from the smaller knight's younger male pilot.  
    "Yea, you? Don't normally see a pair out here on their own?"  
    "Lost our link in the last fight a day ago, we were laying low till we could link up with friendly units before we got picked off." The woman replied as the older man chuckled happily on the comm, interrupting her a little.  
    "Well lucky for you Missy we happen to not be hunting Wolfen Knights\! We heard that there had been reports of Myrmidon Hunter Killer team out here looking for stragglers, so you best head east, you'll link up with the rest of our company there." The old man advised his junior as the knight's arrowhead nodded in gesture.  
    "Be safe brother." The larger machine bellowed to his mechanical comrade as a sign of mutual appreciation and respect for fighting the good fight with them.  
    "Aye, we will head east, thank you, be careful, We had run into at least a company out here so stay low and keep your Halo in passive." The Knight replied to his larger comrade before the duo would turn to walk off as the smallest knight waved them off before the scouting pair continued on their way East.
