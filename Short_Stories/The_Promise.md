# `The Promise`

`"You can't be serious!? Sacrificing yourself?! For what, those mortals?! You could do so much to save them if you stay here! With me! **PLEASE**" The wolf begged, staring into that inky blackness that surrounded and smothered his glowing light, the clearest and sharpest his form has ever been as he begged for the one who laid inside of the darkness.`  
`"Vu... I know... but... but if I stand aside and let billions of mortal creatures... *our* children **die**. I fear this would be the one mistake I would never be able to come back from... I know... Deep within my being that one day, one of those mortals will be able to be the savior you desire.... Until then... Promise me..." A feminine voice replied softly from the void of darkness that surrounded the One of Light.`  
`"No! I can't-!" He snapped back, trying to argue with the darkness, pleading with her to change her mind.`  
`"Promise me!" She snapped with a stern and strong voice that was rarely used.`  
`"Promise me... that you will protect them... be the Guiding Light that I always knew you could be for them. Show what I cannot. Foster their growth and warm their soil and homes." A soft black hand reached out from the darkness and cupped the wolf's muzzle, comforting him as he tried to speak but held his tongue for a moment, trying to gather the words he wanted to say but came up short.`  
`"Now Please... Do the thing you love to do..." The woman continued to speak before he replied.`  
`"Use your light to show the mortals that even monsters have nowhere to hide." She chuckled softly before pulling her hand back.`  
`"I hate you..." The wolf began to cry as he ducked his head down, letting little stars of light drip from his face into the darkness below before sucking in a hard breath of air, screaming out into the air with rage and hatred for those that had pushed them this far. His light blowing out into a blinding aura consuming all of the darkness around him.`
