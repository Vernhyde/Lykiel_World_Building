# The Maneuver

    The men sat hunkered down in the rubble of collapsed structures of varying heights as they waited, overlooking a long stretch of city street. A weapons team waiting at the end of the street up in the second floor of the remains of an old bakery. The men seated deep away from the windows to make them harder to seat. A large HMG resting on a couple tables while on it's tripod as a way to keep it high enough to see through the window. It's belt loaded and ready as they waited. Another element was waiting on the lower levels below a little ways down on the right side of the road, the ambush waiting for their prey.  
    A local ISR unit had reported that a small patrol was on route through the area but the interference from counter surveillance operations and their drones being spiked out of the sky by SHORAD and Wyverns meant they had limited info on the unit. Just that it was 1 light skinned transport and a squad on foot in the lead. The ID and equipment was unable to be confirmed so caution was advised. It would be a careful trap as they did not want to have another case of blue on blue. It was due to that, that indirect support was limited in close danger situations, they had lost 2 other units to misdirected fire and misidentified units. This allowed the enemy to more open with their movements as they were less likely to have arty hammering at their positions, especially with ISR limited on both sides.  
    Eventually the patrol would round the corner at the end of the road, the infantry at the lead hugging the sides of the road, up against the buildings as they slowly moved. A three man team taking the lead down the center of the road, it seemed like they were keeping watch for any mines or debris for the transport truck behind to maneuver around. The canvas cover hiding what might be inside of it. The man in the lead holding a firm grip on the pommel of his sword as it hung around his waist looking down the center of the road at the bakery. His two followers watching the sides of the road as they scanned down it.  
    The HMG team hunkered down and held their breath as their NCO looked carefully through another window with his binoculars to try and ID this unit coming at them. As soon as he saw the three in the lead he muttered a curse to himself. He would be able to immediately identify that the man in the lead was a Mage, pretty distinctive by their larger bladed weapon and lack of rifle as well as the confidence that came with the idea that simple guns could not hurt a man of his stature which may be true for smaller arms as the shimmering nearly see through sphere encircled him being disturbed lightly by the slow moving dust as it passed through without being impeded. The team would prepare to show that the confidence was certainly misguided.  
    The NCO would wait to give the word letting the enemy walk closer and closer into the ambush before he noticed the mage stop, adjusting his grip on the blade, just barely inside the perfect position as he grabs the grip.  
    "Shit! Open up now!" He yelled with the raising of his voice immediately telling the heavy gun to chatter away, dust immediately kicking up as the rounds rip down the street smashing into the Ward of the mage as his two followers dive for cover at the sides of the street. Sparks flying off the ward as rounds were deflected and smashed against the barrier. The rest of the ambush needing to angle themselves a little in their position to fire further down the street than they planned. The mage immediately moving to cover the rest of his unit drawing his sword as smoke suddenly billowed out into the street in front of him, the truck behind pulling off to the side as the entire enemy unit attempts to fight through the deadly ambush, rounds smashing into the walls of the bakery as most of the fire attempted to silence the HMG.  
    The secondary element drew additional weapons as a disposable shoulder fired launcher suddenly blasted into the smoke shooting a massive cone of fire along with a burst of air, throwing the swirling smoke into the air as the street was covered in fire. As soon as the smoke cleared though the mage immediately retaliated as he shot a high energy bolt of electricity down the street, smashing into the bakery as it blew apart a section of the1 building apart the HMG inside falling silent. The mage immediately turning to blast the building next to him as fire was blown away from him, the smell of burnt flesh heavy in the air as a portion of his unit was burned to a crisp by the launcher.  
    Before he would be able to muster another blast though the HMG began to rattle again smashing into his ward throwing more sparks through the air as he was staggered thinking he had dealt with the gun already and his ward was now failing, small arms now ripping into his ward though much less effective made it much harder to think and see as the sphere suddenly was smashed through. A round blowing through the man's side as soon small arms was able to get through, shredding the threat as his blade clanged against the ground. His body being thoroughly dosed with lead before the firing slowed and then stopped. The ambush finishing any stranglers that had survived the fiery blast before a man came out into the street to go for the mage's sword, a nice trophy that could be used later.
