`Knights/Golems`  
	`They will have a dynamic with Mortals as an independent people of sorts. Some will work with mortals and join their societies and militaries to be one of them if they are allowed to. While some will travel as nomads offering their services as a Freelancer and Mercenary, allowing them to spend a few decades with said people before moving on to continue their free travels. These Freelancers are both appreciated by those in need and held with some suspicion by those who do not. Due to the risk of having such powerful though potentially hostile or spying units in their vulnerable cities. This leads to them having quite the mixed relations with mortals which is even more situational depending on the Knight themselves and their personalities like any Mortal-Mortal interaction.`
