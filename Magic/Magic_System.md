# `Magic Fundamentals`

`Micro, Mid and Macro Script are the three forms of magic, all based on the principle of Photo-lithography & Geometric Resonance. Magic isn't triggered by a person speaking a word; it is triggered by the literal geometric shape that energy travels through.`

`Real computer microchips are made using photo-lithography (etching complex, microscopic mazes onto silicon). In Lykiel, if you etch electricity-conducting channels into specific geometric patterns, the energy flowing through them twists into a magical field.`

# `Fundamental Theory: Topological Dynamics`

`Topological Dynamics is the physical science governing arcane fields. Magic is not a supernatural force; it is an emergent physical field induced by running electrical or biological energy through specific geometric paths.`

## `Mechanics`

* **`Geometric Induction:`** `Passing current through a physical wire creates a magnetic field. Similarly, running current through a calibrated geometric script induces an arcane displacement field.`  
* **`Energetic Dissipations:`** `Arcane fields follow standard thermodynamic laws. Field generation produces massive waste heat when unfocused and produces energetic disruptions across the local topological spectrum which are detectable by other field sensitive systems.`  
* **`Substrate Wear:`** `High current throughput wears down microscopic conductive channels over time, causing cheap or heavily used script arrays to burn out.`  
* **`Passive and Parasitic Transduction:`** `While active, high-yield projection strictly requires living neural flux or an awakened golem core, two closed-loop exceptions operate without active caster input.`  
  * **`Ambient Resonant Micro-Logic:`** `Sub-millimeter Micro-Scripts arranged in closed, non-projective logic loops draw micro-currents from background topological radiation. This powers internal calculation and state switching without projecting external physical fields.`  
  * **`Parasitic Inversion:`** `Inscribed defense matrices remain completely inert until struck by an external arcane vector. The incoming spell's bio-topological energy is captured by the array's geometry, which instantly redirects the force into a counter-dispersal wave to neutralize the attack.`

## `Limitations`

* **`Substrate Degradation:`** `High-amperage throughput across microscopic traces induces electromigration and physical wear, causing cheap or heavily used script arrays to burn out or fracture.`  
* **`Geometric Tolerance:`** `Etch tolerances require nanometer-scale precision. Any physical compromise—such as mechanical shock, thermal warping, or ballistic spalling—collapses the field geometry instantly.`  
* **`Thermal Budgets:`** `Energized high-density arrays draw massive currents, establishing a thermal floor that necessitates dedicated heat-sinking or phase-change cooling to prevent hardware failure.`  
* **`Harmonic Interference:`** `Unshielded geometric arrays placed in close proximity create spatial noise, causing circuit shorting or catastrophic field collapse.`

## `Prerequisites`

* **`Precision Substrates:`** `High-purity conductive substrates (silicon, gallium arsenide, or specialized composite wafers) capable of sustaining high current densities without premature structural degradation.`  
* **`Photolithographic Infrastructure:`** `Industrial-grade etching facilities capable of producing micro- and nano-scale geometric line-widths for Micro-Scripts.`  
* **`Active Conduit / Power Feed:`** `Standard conductive arrays remain completely inert until energized by an active conduit—such as an attached biological mage or an autonomous golem chassis power core.`

## `Technological Applications`

* **`Slates:`** `Handheld computational devices operating on a dual-rail architecture. Conventional binary motherboards and lithium-ion batteries power the operating system, display, and standard I/O for mundane users. The embedded micro-script layer utilizes passive ambient resonance to execute high-density calculations, data encryption, and local sensory tasks continuously without requiring a mage operator.`  
* **`Signal Interference & Detection:`** `High-yield arcane applications emit distinct sensory and topological signatures. Passive sensory assets and specialized mages can detect and track energized arrays across significant distances, whereas unaugmented conventional systems remain sensor-neutral.`

## `Historical Context`

* `Early arcane development relied on macroscopic, hand-tooled channels (e.g., silver or copper inlays routed manually into non-conductive stone or dense hardwoods). While functional, spatial field density was extremely low, requiring room-scale floor plans to project basic fields. The transition to solid-state chemistry and photolithographic silicon etching compressed these arrays into compact footprints, enabling man-portable foci, computational Slates, and weapon-integrated munitions.`

### `Baseline System Rules (Quick Reference)`

* **`Power Dependency:`** `Script arrays are completely inert geometry until energized by an active source (a living mage or a Golem Core).`  
* **`Thermal Limits:`** `Converting energy into magic generates heavy waste heat, requiring cooling for high-density hardware.`  
* **`Physical Wear:`** `High energy throughput slowly degrades etched channels, leading to eventual script burnout or hardware failure.`

# `Biological Expression: Neural Architecture`

**`Neural Architecture`** `defines the biological mutation that enables living organisms to internally generate, throttle, and direct arcane potential.`

## `Mechanics`

* **`Fractal Neural Clusters:`** `A rare genetic mutation embeds microscopic, geometric neural networks into the brain and spine. These act as biological circuit arrays.`  
* **`Somatic Casting:`** `Mages convert biological calories and electrical nervous impulses into magic. Energy can be released in three ways`  
  * *`Raw / Rough:`* `Unshaped, high-entropy discharge with heavy thermal waste.`  
  * *`Refined:`* `Directly focused through the body along a simple linear path.`  
  * *`Focused:`* `Routed into an external script array for complex, automated execution.`  
* **`Metabolic Fueling:`** `Arcane output consumes biological calories at an accelerated rate. Mages can utilize Neural Mana Pathways via medical transdermal ports to inject calorie-dense nutrient-fluid directly into the digestive and nervous systems during sustained operational deployments.`

## `Limitations`

* **`Neural Burnout:`** `Channeling energy beyond biological tolerance, or attempting complex calculations without an external focus, induces severe neural back-pressure. Neural Burnout physically scars or destroys Fractal Neural Clusters, resulting in irreversible loss of casting ability, neurological deficits, or death.`  
* **`Thermal & Spatial Backflow:`** `High-energy projections produce internal thermal and kinetic stress, causing localized internal burns, severe fevers, or systemic tissue damage if directional containment is compromised.`  
* **`Cognitive Complexity Barrier:`** `The human brain cannot natively maintain multi-stage execution loops, conditional triggers, or spatial persistence without acute cognitive fatigue.`  
* **`Unassisted (Substrate-Free) Ceilings:`** `Operating entirely without an external script medium enforces hard tactical boundaries:`  
  * *`Effective Range:`* `0 to 20 meters maximum effective distance.`  
  * *`Complexity Ceiling:`* `Linear, single-phase direct output only (no branching logic, automated target tracking, or secondary trigger phases).`  
  * *`Efficiency Loss:`* `High metabolic waste and severe energetic dissipation. Unfocused casters (colloquially termed Burners) exhaust caloric reserves rapidly and risk accelerated burnout.`

## `Prerequisites`

* **`Genetic Mutation:`** `Presence of functional, undamaged Fractal Neural Clusters along the central nervous system.`  
* **`Mental Syntax Training:`** `Rigorous biological conditioning required to regulate heart rate, core temperature, and neural pulse frequency to fire pathways safely without self-immolation.`

## `Historical Context`

* `Historically labeled as volatile anomalies or "Wild Traces," early mages had a mortality rate exceeding 70% before adulthood due to spontaneous combustion and catastrophic burnout. Modern clinical training and safety dampers transformed volatile subjects into standardized assets.`

## `Innate Predisposition Families`

`A mage's neural cluster geometry predetermines their baseline transduction vector. Learning pathways outside this innate predisposition requires forcing energy through dormant tissue to stimulate micro-neural growth.`

* **`Thermodynamic Transduction:`** `Thermodynamics: Temperature manipulation (combustion/pyro-generation or freezing/cryo-extraction). (Note: Replaces the baseline Destructive Thermal class.)`  
* **`Kinetic:`** `Direct force vectors (directional push or pull).`  
* **`Bio-Electrical:`** `Conversion of metabolic energy into electricity or plasma discharge.`  
* **`Acousto-Vibrational:`** `Conversion of somatic pulses into atmospheric compression waves. Lacking an external focus, this expression is imprecise: an unassisted mage can produce high-decibel concussive blasts or damp all acoustic transmission within a direct radius, but cannot execute selective, localized acoustic abilities such as muting a single person.`  
* **`Sensory / Topological Interception (Seers):`** `A rare genetic variant where clusters operate as passive receivers attuned to topological signal interference. Seers detect distant energetic fluctuations or have other means of detecting forces, active script usage, or structural anomalies, but possess minimal capacity for high-yield destructive projection without fatal cluster burnout.`

# 	`Abiotic Expression: Golem Core Dynamics & Lifecycle`

`Abiotic Expression defines non-organic life forms animated by Topological Saturation (arcane radiation), resulting in self-organizing fractal lattices within non-biological substrates.`

## `Mechanics & Emergence`

* **`Topological Crystallization:`** `Sustained exposure to high-density ambient flux forces conductive mineral compounds and micro-etched substrates to reorganize into self-sustaining fractal pathways.`  
* **`Abiotic Conduit Status:`** `An awakened core generates autonomous Bio-Topological Flux identical in functional frequency to a biological mage's central nervous system, allowing golems to channel, throttle, and execute scripts without human intervention.`

## `Classification Hierarchy`

* **`Feral / Primitive`**  
  * `Substrate Baseline: Quartz, iron silicates, river stone, scrap metals.`  
  * `Lattice Density: Coarse Macro-Lattice (millimeter scale).`  
  * `Arcane & Cognitive Capability: Sub-sapient; low-yield kinetic or thermal discharge; lacks complex logic.`  
  * `Behavioral Profile: Instinctive, territorial, and animalistic. Driven by flux consumption and ambient territorial defense.`  
* **`Machined Frame`**  
  * `Substrate Baseline: Standard factory-etched logic boards and industrial machinery.`  
  * `Lattice Density: Unstructured Mid-Lattice.`  
  * `Arcane & Cognitive Capability: Procedural task execution; limited conditional logic; unstable long-term field control.`  
  * `Behavioral Profile: Equivalent to pack animals or automated factory equipment; prone to logic decay without routine calibration.`  
* **`Knight-Class`**  
  * `Substrate Baseline: Cleanroom photolithography stacks, computational Slates, and dedicated mech chassis.`  
  * `Lattice Density: High-Density Nano-Lattice (nanometer-scale baseline).`  
  * `Arcane & Cognitive Capability: Fully sapient; autonomous multi-phase script execution; high flux output.`  
  * `Behavioral Profile: Fully sentient combat partners with Titan-class autonomy. Possesses distinct tactical cognition, voice output, and unique psychological traits.`

## `Lifecycle & Propagation`

* **`Primitive Fission (Lattice Fission)`**  
  * `Coarse mineral golems continuously absorb ambient radiation. When physical stress fractures the over-saturated crystal node, the detached fragment retains a crude energetic charge and accretes surrounding loose stone and scrap to form a distinct juvenile animate.`  
* **`Sapient Propagation: The Foundry Nest`**  
  * `Knight-Class cores do not depend on mortal industrial facilities for survival. Instead, independent or wild Knight units establish isolated Foundry Nests to birth offspring.`  
    * `Nest Construction: Golems construct hermetically sealed environmental chambers within deep caverns, ruins, or hollowed stone vaults. By maintaining a continuous electrostatic barrier across the entry thresholds, they eliminate dust, moisture, and particulate contamination, creating an abiotic cleanroom.`  
    * `Substrate Smelting & Zone-Refining: Utilizing coordinated thermal and kinetic vectors, parent golems melt raw quartz, silica-rich sands, and conductive ore veins. They pass focused magnetic fields through the molten material to pull out impurities, zone-refining ultra-pure crystalline silicon boules and thin wafers without mortal mechanical tooling.`  
    * `Inductive Epitaxy: Within the Nest cradle, the parent tethers directly to the prepared wafer. Over a gestation period of 4 to 8 weeks, the parent uses somatic kinetic vectors at nanometer tolerances to deposit conductive metal traces atom-by-atom, etching the child core's fractal neural architecture.`  
    * `The Awakening Surge: The parent discharges a calibrated, high-amperage flux pulse into the finished lattice, permanently sacrificing a small fraction of its own maximum energetic capacity to trigger autonomous life in the offspring.`

	`Personality Drift: Micro-scale thermodynamic fluctuations during the manual epitaxy process ensure that no two child cores are identical. Each emerges with distinct cognitive quirks, tactical instincts, and vocal timbres.`

## `Metabolic Sustenance`

* `Passive Radiotrophic Absorption (Basal Demand)`  
  * `Golems utilize conductive dermal plating and surface nodes to absorb ambient background radiation. In areas with standard-to-high mana density, this trickle-charge maintains cognitive functions, sensors, and low-speed locomotion indefinitely without fuel.`

## `Limitations & Vulnerabilities`

* `Nest Vulnerability & Incubation Drain`  
  * `A Foundry Nest requires massive environmental stability. Disrupting the electrostatic seal during the 4-to-8-week gestation introduces micro-particulates that ruin the growing core. Additionally, the parent suffers a 40% reduction in combat output and kinetic reaction speed while channeling flux to the seedling.`  
* `Resource Depletion`  
  * `Creating a sapient core consumes vast quantities of high-purity crystalline quartz and rare conductive trace elements. Golems must actively mine, scavenge, or trade for refined metals to sustain their lineage.`  
* `Core Starvation & Logic Drift`  
  * `Sustained operation in mana-depleted dead zones forces cores into low-power torpor. If forced to maintain high cognitive activity without radiotrophic absorption or chemical intake, the lattice suffers irreversible micro-fracturing, leading to permanent memory corruption, personality degradation, or core death.`

# `Industrial Standard: The Script Standard & Computational Symbiosis`

## `The Computational Framework`

`Modern arcane deployment operates on a strict Hardware/Software Input-Output Model:`

* **`The Mage (CPU / Hardware):`** `Generates raw power and controls the energy flow. Mages cannot process complex targeting or delayed triggers in their minds without risking cognitive burnout.`  
* **`The Script or Focus (Software / Circuitry):`** `Physical geometric etching handles the math and logic (such as automated triggers, guided paths, or shape control). The mage supplies energy, while the script determines what the spell does.`   
* **`Non-Mage Usability:`** `Workshops and factories can mass-produce script geometry on gear or munitions via photolithography. These scripts stay inert until powered by a mage or a Golem Core.`

## `Mechanics`

* **`Processing Offload:`** `Channeling energy into an engraved Focus or script array offloads complex spatial calculations away from biological tissue. The physical array routes the energy through pre-calculated geometric logic, enabling multi-stream manipulation, trajectory bending, and phase-delayed execution.`  
* **`Geometric Damping:`** `Passing power through an external focus absorbs energetic back-pressure, functioning as a circuit fuse that prevents destructive feedback from reaching the caster's nervous system.`  
* **`Transient Etching (The Trace):`** `Mages can apply field-expedient scripts using high-conductivity mediums (e.g., metallic ink pens, reactive chemical aerosols, or rapid-cure conductive resins) to form temporary arrays on operational surfaces.`  
* **`Sustained Loops & Traps:`** `Delayed-action scripts, proximity wards, and area-denial fields do not possess internal power reservoirs. They rely on an active energetic feed from a sustaining mage. If the anchor mage is killed or incapacitated, the energetic circuit collapses, causing all linked dormant scripts to neutralize instantly.`

## `Industrial Interoperability`

* `Non-mages, automated manufacturing lines, and precision workshops can mass-produce, mill, and chemically stamp geometric scripts onto ordnance, armor, and equipment via photolithography and other methods.`  
* **`Activation Requirement:`** `Mundane-fabricated script arrays remain completely inert until energized by an active conduit—either an attached biological mage or the internal power core of an autonomous golem chassis.`

# `Master Glossary & Nomenclature Index`

## `Script Classifications`

* **`Macro-Script:`** `Large-format geometric arrays etched across meter-scale surfaces (fortifications, artillery emplacements, vehicle hulls, heavy missile warhead liners).`  
* **`Mid-Script:`** `Millimeter-to-centimeter scale geometric arrays precision-engraved onto firearms, tactical gear, focus conduits, and weapon receivers.`  
* **`Micro-Script:`** `Sub-millimeter and nanometer-scale runic arrays produced via precision photo-lithography on computational logic boards and Slates.`  
* **`Sub-Script:`** `Modular geometric sub-routines that link to form complex commands, conditional filters, or variable spell vectors.`

## `System Terminology`

* **`Topological Dynamics:`** `The academic and physical science defining magical geometry and field interaction.`  
* **`Neural Architecture:`** `The biological discipline and physiological structures enabling somatic casting.`  
* **`Script Standard:`** `The industrial and military framework governing standardized geometric array fabrication and usage.`  
* **`Fractal Neural Clusters (Clusters):`** `The mutated biological nodes integrated into the brain and central nervous system of mages.`  
* **`Neural Burnout:`** `Permanent physiological degradation or destruction of Fractal Neural Clusters caused by energetic over-draw, backflow, or computational overload.`  
* **`Burner:`** `Colloquial term for a mage operating without a focus, characterized by low operational efficiency, limited range, and high risk of fatal burnout.`  
* **`The Etch / The Trace:`** `Informal slang denoting etched geometric arrays and temporary field-inscriptions.`  
* **`Bio-Topological Flux:`** `The precise bio-resonant electrical current produced exclusively by Fractal Neural Clusters and awakened Golem Cores, serving as the required carrier wave to induce arcane fields through geometric scripts.`  
* **`Golem Core:`** `An abiotic lattice node formed from mineral crystallization or photolithographic silicon that has achieved autonomous bio-topological resonance.`  
* **`Capacitance Decay:`** `The rapid dissipation rate (typically 30–45 seconds) of topological charge stored within un-tethered single-use munitions.`  
* **`Inductive Epitaxy:`** `The process by which a sapient Knight-Class Golem seeds, engraves, and jump-starts an unprogrammed silicon substrate to produce an autonomous offspring core.`  
* **`Radiotrophy:`** `The passive biological and mechanical process of absorbing ambient topological radiation through surface arrays to maintain basal metabolic energy.`  
* **`Foundry Nest:`** `An isolated golem cleanroom using electrostatic barriers for core fabrication.`  
* **`Lattice Fission:`** `The primitive, non-sapient reproductive mechanism wherein an over-saturated coarse mineral core physically fractures under stress, yielding an energetic fragment that accretes local matter into an autonomous juvenile animate.`  
* **`Personality Drift:`** `The divergence of cognitive quirks, tactical habits, and vocal timbres in offspring cores caused by micro-scale thermodynamic fluctuations during the manual Inductive Epitaxy process.`  
* **`Abiotic Expression:`** `The categorization of non-biological life forms animated by sustained topological saturation, characterized by self-organizing fractal lattices capable of generating autonomous Bio-Topological Flux.`
