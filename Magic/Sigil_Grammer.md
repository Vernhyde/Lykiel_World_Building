# Sigil Grammar & Geometric Syntax

**Sigil Grammar** is the geometric rule-set used to design magic scripts under Topological Dynamics. Magic is not triggered by spoken words; it happens when electrical or biological current runs through specific geometric shapes. Every script—from hand-drawn floor traps to microscopic Slate processors—is built from five core parts: Core Polygons, Internal Sub-Parameters, Vector Staves, Sub-Script Modifiers, and a Bounding Girdle.

## Mechanics

* **Current Flow:** When powered by a mage or a Golem Core, energy moves through a continuous circuit in four steps:  
  * **Input Terminal:** Power enters through a physical contact pad (the center of a circle, a metal grip on a Focus, or a wire plug on a missile).  
  * **Core Polygon & Sub-Parameters:** Current loops through the center shape. The corners bend the current to set the spell's domain (Force, Heat, etc.), while small inner bridges (Sub-Parameters) fine-tune what it targets.  
  * **Vector Staves & Sub-Scripts:** Energy travels out along straight or angled lines (Vector Staves) and passes through notched logic marks (Sub-Scripts) that control timing, triggers, and target filters.  
  * **Bounding Girdle:** The spell either fires out through a gap in the outer ring (Aperture) or spreads evenly across the outer ring.  
* **The Vertex Rule:** Each corner (vertex) in a Core Polygon bends the current at a specific angle. Sharp angles (3 or 4 corners) violently shear the current into raw force or heat. Wide angles (7 to 10 corners) create high-frequency waves that affect living cells, gravity, atomic bonds, or data.  
* **Polygon Orientation & Stave Attachment (Emit vs. Absorb):**  
  * *Point-Fed / Corner-Spoked (Emit State):* When the input line or radial Vector Staves attach directly to the sharp corners (vertices) of the polygon, the shape pushes, emits, or amplifies energy outward.  
  * *Flat-Fed / Wall-Spoked (Absorb State):* When the input line or radial Vector Staves attach to the flat edges (midpoints) of the polygon—leaving the corners unattached—the shape pulls, absorbs, or dampens energy inward.  
* **Line Width (The Busbar Rule):** Wider and deeper lines carry more power without melting. Nanometer Micro-Scripts only handle low-power data (Slates and Rune-Tags), while high-power combat scripts (Mid-Scripts and Macro-Scripts) need thick metal channels exposed on the outside of gun barrels, shields, and armor tiles.  
* **Physical Byproducts:** Firing a high-power script creates visible and physical waste. Active lines glow, distort the air with heat, hum, and smell like ozone. Overloading a script (Substrate Exhaustion) shatters silicon wafers or melts metal lines into scorched, empty grooves.

## Limitations

* **Angle Precision & Damage:** Simple shapes (3 to 5 corners) allow a wide margin of error ($\pm {2}^{\circ }$) and can be drawn quickly by hand (The Trace). Complex shapes (7 to 10 corners) have wide, shallow corners ($\geq 12{8}^{\circ }$) that need tight precision ($\pm 0.{1}^{\circ }$). Heat warping, bullet damage, or a scratch across a line breaks the circuit.  
* **The Circle Short-Circuit ($11+$ Corners):** A true circle has no corners and cannot bend current into a domain. Past 10 corners ($>14{4}^{\circ }$), mortal metals and silicon cannot keep the corners sharp enough at an atomic level. The current reads 11+ corners as a circle, causing an instant short-circuit that vaporizes the script.  
* **Efficiency Loss:** Simple shapes convert power efficiently (~85% for a 3-sided Triad). Complex shapes and long modifier chains create high resistance, turning 70–80% of the input power into waste heat.  
* **Line Crossing (Harmonic Interference):** Two active lines cannot cross each other on a flat surface without shorting out. Complex spells must either chain separate circles in a row or stack layers separated by ceramic or silicon insulation.

## Prerequisites

* **Machining vs. Photolithography:** Any mortal shape (3 to 10 corners) can be cut at medium or large scale using machine tools, protractors, or metal stencils. Cleanroom Photo-lithography is only needed to pack millions of microscopic shapes onto Slate chips and Golem Cores.  
* **Material Quality:** Simple scripts (3 to 6 corners) work on rough stone, wood, or steel. Complex scripts (7 to 10 corners) need smooth, pure materials (copper, silver, ceramic, or pure silicon) so rough grain doesn't ruin the shallow angles.  
* **Power Source:** All scripts are dead metal or ink until powered by a living mage or a Golem Core (except Decad micro-logic chips that run on background radiation).

#### **Historical Context**

* Early cultures discovered 3-, 4-, and 5-sided shapes first because they could be carved by hand, limiting ancient magic to force, fire/ice, and lightning. Shapes with 6 to 10 corners originally required massive temple floor plans measured by master masons. Modern machining and Photo-lithography shrank these complex shapes onto portable weapons, medical gear, and Slates.

## I. Core Polygons (Vertices 3 to $\infty$)

The center shape sets the spell's domain, ordered from simple physical forces to complex concepts:

#### **Phase 1: Energy Domains (3 to 6 Corners)**

* **3 Corners — Triad (Triangle): Kinetic Domain** ($6{0}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Physical force, push/pull vectors, shockwaves, and sound/seismic vibrations.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed pushes outward or blasts shockwaves (Magical Reactive Armor); Flat-Fed pulls inward or absorbs impact.  
* **4 Corners — Tetrad (Square / Diamond): Thermodynamic Domain** ($9{0}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Heat and cold transfer.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed (Diamond) pulls heat out (freezing, equipment cooling); Flat-Fed (Square) adds heat (fire, melting, plasma heating).  
* **5 Corners — Pentad (Pentagon): Electromagnetic Domain** ($10{8}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Electricity and magnetism.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed fires lightning arcs and EMPs; Flat-Fed creates magnetic fields (pinching hot gas into Plasma Needles), rail propulsion, and static barriers (Foundry Nests).  
* **6 Corners — Hexad (Hexagon): Radiative Domain** ($12{0}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Light and radiation (infrared, visible light, UV, and hard radiation).  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed fires lasers, blinding flashes, or radiation; Flat-Fed bends light (optical camouflage) or blocks radiation.

#### **Phase 2: Matter & Mass Domains (7 to 9 Corners)**

* **7 Corners — Heptad (Heptagon): Biotic Domain** ($128.5{7}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Living cells, blood, nerves, and metabolism.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed speeds up clotting, tissue repair, and nutrient absorption (Neural Mana Pathways); Flat-Fed numbs nerves, pauses cell growth, or causes tissue death.  
  * *Field Use:* Used by medics via engraved medical Foci or factory-stamped disposable bandages charged by a mage or a chemical battery pack.  
* **8 Corners — Octad (Octagon): Gravimetric Domain** ($13{5}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Weight, mass, and inertia.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed increases weight and downward gravity; Flat-Fed lowers weight and dampens recoil or heavy Knight-Class golem movement.  
* **9 Corners — Nonad (Nonagon): Atomic Domain** ($14{0}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Chemical and metallic bonds, rust, and material purity.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed weakens bonds (causing rust, melting, brittle armor, or purging impurities in Foundry Nests); Flat-Fed strengthens bonds to harden armor and alloys.

#### **Phase 3: Information & Divine Domains (10+ Corners)**

* **10 Corners — Decad (Decagon): Information Domain** ($14{4}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* The highest mortal domain. Processes data, sensors, and logic using background radiation without creating physical force.  
  * *Orientation:* Point-Fed sends and intercepts sensor pings (Seer tracking); Flat-Fed runs computer logic (Slates, Rune-Tag IFF, and Golem Cores).  
* **11+ Corners ($\geq 11\rightarrow \infty$): Spatial-Temporal & Divine Magic** ($\geq 147.2{7}^{\circ }$)  
  * *Effect:* Bends space and time (portals, sealing Jasakii rifts, creating matter).  
  * *Mortal Limit:* Mortal materials cannot handle 11+ corners without short-circuiting. Only Gods (like Vuhaldir) and cosmic anomalies can cast these by folding geometry directly into space.

## II. Internal Sub-Parameters (Fine-Tuning Bridges)

When a script uses a double-walled Core Polygon, small connecting lines—called Sub-Parameters—are etched between the inner and outer walls. These bridges tune the shape to target a specific material or tissue instead of firing full-spectrum.

#### **Standard Bridge Marks**

* **Solid Bar (│):** Targets light, fast, or fluid states (air/liquids, blood, direct current, infrared, weight, rust, basic pings).  
* **Gapped Bar (─||─):** Targets elastic, soft, or pulsed states (ballistic impacts, soft tissue/skin, magnetic fields, visible light, recoil damping, metal hardness, Slate logic).  
* **Sawtooth Bridge (─∿─):** Targets dense, hard, or complex states (stone/steel shattering, solid metal heating, bone repair, static barriers, UV/radiation, silicon refining, Golem Core syntax).  
* **Forked Bridge (─<─):** Targets boundaries and nerve synapses (pain blocking and Neural Mana Pathways).

## III. Vector Staves (Aim & Shape)

**Vector Staves** are the lines running from the Core Polygon's corners to the outer ring. They control the spell's direction, range, and wave pattern:

* **Axial Stave (Single Straight Line):** Exits through a gap in the outer ring to fire a tight beam or jet (rifles, lances, Plasma Needles). Longer lines create tighter beams.  
* **Forked Stave (Y Split):** Splits the spell into a wide cone or multi-stream fan.  
* **Radial Staves (Wheel Spokes):** Lines running from all corners to the outer ring, creating a $36{0}^{\circ }$ pulse or dome (mines, armor tiles, area wards).  
* **Oscillating Stave (Zig-Zag /\/\):** Pulses the energy into waves, turning Kinetic (3) force into soundwaves/silence or tuning Radiative (6) light.  
* **Stepped Stave (Square Coil):** Folded $9{0}^{\circ }$ switchbacks that pack high pressure into a tiny space for contact tools (breaching rams, medical plates).

## IV. Sub-Script Modifiers (Triggers & Filters)

**Sub-Scripts** are small marks etched onto the staves or outer ring that act as automatic triggers and timers:

* **Capacitive Gate (||):** Double cross-bars on a stave that hold the mage's charge inside the shape until triggered (used in mines and primed missiles during their 30–45s Capacitance Decay window).  
* **Inversion Spurs (V):** Inward-pointing chevrons around the outer ring that catch incoming enemy magic and funnel it into the center shape to fuel a counter-blast (Magical Reactive Armor tiles).  
* **Step-Up / Step-Down Ring:** A second polygon drawn inside the main shape (Step-Up) to boost power for a single flash, or outside (Step-Down) to lower power for a long, steady effect.  
* **Phase Bridge (~):** A zig-zag resistor line connecting two separate circles that delays the second circle by a set time.  
* **Resonance Notch (Y):** A side spur linked to a microscopic Decad (10) node that reads Rune-Tag IFF signals and shuts the trap off if an ally steps into range.  
* **Anchor Latch (◊):** A terminal loop that lets a mage feed power to a trap from a distance; if the mage dies or disconnects, the trap shuts off.

## V. The Bounding Girdle (Outer Frame & Cooling)

The Bounding Girdle is the outer border that holds the spell together and vents heat:

* **Closed Girdle (Solid Ring):** An unbroken outer ring that keeps energy inside (Slates and Rune-Tags) or releases an even $36{0}^{\circ }$ burst when triggered (mines and MRA tiles).  
* **Aperture Girdle (Gapped Ring):** An outer ring with an open gap at the front, acting like a gun barrel to force all energy out in one direction.  
* **Rounded Polygon Frames:** Armor tiles and patches can use triangle, square, or hexagon outer borders instead of circles, as long as the outer corners are smoothly rounded so they don't act like a second polygon.  
* **Thermal Bleed Fins (|||)**: Short radiator lines along the outer ring that pull waste heat away from the center shape so the script doesn't melt.

## VI. Spell Layouts & Compound Examples

Complex spells combine the eight mortal shapes (3 to 10) using four layouts:

1. **Concentric (Rings inside Rings):** Compact and symmetrical for flat armor tiles, shields, and Slates, but traps heat in the middle and must be pre-made in a factory.  
2. **Satellite (Center Circle + Orbiting Sub-Circles):** Spreads heat out cleanly on shells, medical plates, and Golem Cores.  
3. **Linear Chain (Circles in a Row):** Connected end-to-end by Phase Bridges (~). Fits narrow gun barrels and lets field Etchers chain small pocket stencils together on the fly.  
4. **Branching Network (Split & Merge Tree):** One circle splits into multiple parallel circles to fire a wide fan (like a directional mine). Merging lines must be the same length so the currents hit at the same time without shorting out.

#### **Compound Spell Examples**

* **"Cryo-Shear" Breaching Strip (Linear Chain):** Links a Point-Fed Tetrad (4 - Freeze) with Sawtooth Bridges (─∿─ - Solid Metal/Stone) across a 0.4-second Phase Bridge (~) into a Point-Fed Triad (3 - Force) with Zig-Zag Staves (Sound). Freezes a heavy door brittle while venting stolen heat out its Thermal Fins, then shatters the frozen door with a deep bass vibration.  
* **"Vagus-Suture" Medical Patch (Concentric Mandala):** A Point-Fed Heptad (7 - Biotic) with Gapped (─||─ - Soft Tissue) and Solid (│ - Blood) bridges, seven staves with Capacitive Gates (||), and outer Thermal Fins (|||). Clots bleeding and stitches wounds without calcifying bone or burning the patient.  
* **"Plasma Needle" ATGM Warhead (3-Circle Chain):** Links a Tetrad (4 - Heat) + Flat-Fed Pentad (5 - Magnetic Pinch) + Triad (3 - Kinetic Push). Melts a copper cone into ionized gas, pinches it into a tight magnetic needle, and fires it forward to punch through heavy tank armor.  
* **Mind / Core Interfacing:** Combines Heptad (7 - Biotic) + Pentad (5 - Electricity) + Decad (10 - Information) to match how living brains work (flesh + electricity + data) for neural safety dampers. (Golem Cores swap the Heptad (7) for a Nonad (9 - Atomic) mineral lattice).
