## Calendar System

New Leaf - 92 Days  
Bright Sol/Sun - 91 Days  
Falling/Dying Leaf - 91 Days  
Frozen Sol/Sun - 91 Days

365 Day Year  
The New Year starts on the 2nd of New Leaf (The 1st is not considered a New Year due to the New Cycle Celebration)

A New Moon every 4 weeks, with First, Full, Last, and New Moon happening every Week

The Date Format is as follows: Year/Week - Day/Month   
((Casual Example: Year 1/Week 1 - Day 1/New Leaf))  
((Formal Example: 1st Week of Year 1 - 1st Day of New Leaf))  
[People usually state just the Day and Month and occasionally the Week if trying to clarify, they rarely use the Year unless writing a formal document or talking about an exact date]
