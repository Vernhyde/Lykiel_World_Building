`Serpent Unit is a Vashnalli PMC urban raid unit not affiliated with the Lumerian Army, it has attached supporting elements from the Order.`  
`Serpent 1 is composed of a 9 pax with 3 attached.`   
`1 Squad Lead, 1 Fireteam Alpha Lead, 1 Fireteam Bravo Lead, 1 Breaching Mage, 1 Support Mage, 4 Rifles.`   
`3 attached are 1 Order Seer, 1 Order Healer, 1 Order Shaman`

`The white figure in No Knock is an Order Shaman, a powerful mage and Jasakii specialist able to provide additional support, guidance, and protection as needed. They are also the top Order Agent in the detachment.`

`There are 3 Shamans and 1 Light-bearer in the whole unit, 1 Shaman per squad, and the Light-bearer with the HQ element. The Light-bearer is the unit's Order Attache`

`Serpent Platoon is a part of Vashnalli Security Forces. Private Security Contractor, which is based in the city of Shieldhold in the United Kingdoms of Lumeria. They assist with raids in the large shield wall metropolis, working more efficiently and off books compared to the city police, while being more precise than the Lumerian Army. They work with direct support and help from the Order of the Light-Father, which is also based in the city and assists the public, police, and army, as well as anyone dealing with Jasakii threats. With the support of the Order, connections outside of the Order and their Parent Company Vernhyde Holdings, which also controls Leechwood Manufacturing and Kuki Biotech, three of the most powerful companies in the world of Lykiel, gives Vashnalli a lot of autonomy to do as they need when they need to eliminate Jasakii and their Cult Cells.`
