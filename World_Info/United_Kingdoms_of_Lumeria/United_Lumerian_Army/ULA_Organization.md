# `The United Lumerian Army Echelon Blueprint`

`The United Lumerian Army (ULA) operates on a highly organized, geometric hierarchy designed to project federal power seamlessly across the Union. This blueprint lays out the full structural chain of command, from the smallest multi-man fire team to the massive, kingdom-wide Army commands.`

## **`Ⅰ. Fire Team & Squad Level`**

`At the lowest tactical rung, small units execute immediate action drills, securing terrain or crewing single platforms.`

### **`Fire Team / Crew`**

* **`Scale:`** `3–5 Personnel.`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Designated by phonetic alphabet or functional assignment within a squad.`  
* **`Examples:`** `Fire Team Alpha, Fire Team Bravo, Vehicle Crew 1.`

### **`Squad / Section`**

* **`Scale:`** `10–15 Personnel.`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Numbered sequentially with Arabic numerals inside their parent platoon. Can feature functional sub-descriptors depending on their equipment loadout.`  
* **`Examples:`** `1st Infantry Squad, 3rd Heavy Weapons Section, 2nd Combat Engineer Recon Section.`

## **`Ⅱ. Platoon & Company Level`**

`These are the lowest echelons commanded by commissioned officers, functioning as the primary building blocks of battlefield deployment.`

### **`Platoon`**

* **`Scale:`** `40–60 Personnel (e.g., Standard Light Infantry Platoon caps at 58).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Assigned a Roman numeral within the company, often prefaced by the sub-kingdom of origin for clear Identification Friend or Foe (IFF) routing.`  
* **`Structure:`** `Composed of a small Platoon Headquarters node and 3–4 tactical squads.`  
* **`Examples:`** `I Platoon (Shieldhold), III Platoon (Wolfen).`

### **`Company / Battery`**

* **`Scale:`** `150–200 Personnel (e.g., Standard Infantry Company sits at 188).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Designated by a letter designation (Alpha through Delta) or a traditional line title, anchored to their parent battalion.`  
* **`Structure:`** `Consists of a robust Company HQ element, 3 Line Maneuver Platoons, and integrated support assets.`  
* **`Examples:`** `Alpha Company, Bravo Battery (Ballista Armored Tactical Artillery).`

## **`Ⅲ. Battalion to Brigade Level`**

`At this tier, units transition from pure maneuver elements into independent, self-contained tactical forces capable of sustained, multi-day operations.`

### **`Battalion`**

* **`Scale:`** `500–1,000 Personnel (3–5 Line Companies + 1 Headquarters & Support Company).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Sequential Arabic numerals tied to their parent Regiment or functional branch (e.g., 1st Battalion, 3rd Shieldhold Infantry; 2nd Combined Arms Battalion).`  
* **`Role & Doctrine:`** `The basic tactical maneuver element. Deployable across open-field campaigns, urban combat, or static defensive emplacements.`  
* **`The Attachments Pool:`** `Houses specialized organic assets parceled out to companies as needed, including Scout-Sniper Observation Teams (SNOT), EOD, Combat Engineer squads, and tactical Warding/Focus-Script mage cells.`  
* **`Examples:`** `1st Battalion, 3rd Shieldhold Infantry, 4th Systems Battalion (Wolfen).`

### **`Regiment`**

* **`Scale:`** `Variable (Parent identity and administrative umbrella for 2–4 active or reserve Battalions).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Historical lineage, patron founder, or regional sub-kingdom title (e.g., 4th Capital Guard Regiment).`  
* **`Role & Doctrine:`** `Administrative and ceremonial custodians of unit heritage. Maneuver battalions draw their colors, tradition, and recruit pipeline from their Regiment, but deploy operationally under combined-arms Brigades.`  
* **`Example:`** `3rd Shieldhold Infantry Regiment, 4th Capital Guard Regiment, 7th Royal Dragoon Regiment`

### **`Brigade`**

* **`Scale:`** `3,000–5,000 Personnel (3–4 Maneuver Battalions + Organic Support).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Sequential Arabic numerals combined with primary combat doctrine.`  
* **`Structure:`** `A self-contained combined-arms formation integrating line battalions with dedicated combat engineers, field artillery/runic batteries, logistics trains, and medical stations.`  
* **`Employment:`** `Capable of independent deployment across external theaters or occupying major strategic terrain (such as an entire 15–25 km [9–15 miles] sector of Shieldhold's perimeter).`  
* **`Examples:`** `2nd Armored Brigade (Steel Legion), 5th Vanguard Strike Brigade, 1st Capital Guard Brigade.`

## **`Ⅳ. Division & Corps Level`**

`The absolute peak of the ULA command structure, managing massive geographic fronts and coordinating national defense.`

### **`Division`**

* **`Scale:`** `10,000–18,000 Personnel (3–4 Brigades + Divisional Artillery, Aviation, and Sustainment Brigades).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Ordinal Arabic numerals paired with a functional specialization or operational sector.`  
* **`Structure & Doctrine:`** `The primary operational warfighting command, capable of sustained, multi-month campaigns without external supply.`  
* **`Dual Deployment Capability:`**  
  * *`Expeditionary:`* `Deploys as a complete theater asset to break foreign fronts or stabilize allied nations.`  
  * *`Homeland Defense:`* `When stationed domestically, a Division commands an entire metropolitan quadrant or border defense zone, such as the complete northern wall face, interior bulkheads, and ground-level approach corridors. integrating its organic artillery and warding relays into the local infrastructure.`  
* **`Examples:`** `1st Guard Division (Citadel Core), 2nd Wall Division (North Sector), 4th Armored Strike Division.`

### **`Corps`**

* **`Scale:`** `30,000–60,000 Personnel (2–4 Divisions + Corps Separate Brigades).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Formally designated by Roman Numerals, paired with ancestral or operational theater epithets.`  
* **`Structure & Doctrine:`** `Operational-level command coordinating multi-division campaigns. To preserve the structural geometry of military magic and defensive warding anchors, operational deployments are capped at six active Corps per theater.`  
* **`Corps Separate Assets:`** `Controls independent strategic reserves bypassing division control, such as Corps Heavy Artillery, Structural Engineering Battalions, and Elite High-Arcane Strike Wings.`  
* **`Shieldhold Army Force Distribution:`** `As the military of a single sovereign kingdom within Lumeria, the Shieldhold Army organizes its standing corps by strategic domain:`  
  * ***`I Corps (`**`"The Shield Wall"):`* **`The Sovereign Defense Corps. Anchored permanently to the defense of Shieldhold itself, securing the skyscraper-height perimeter walls, the inner citadel, and the surrounding metropolitan approaches.`  
  * ***`II through IV Corps:`*** `Field and Border Corps. Stationed along Shieldhold’s outer national frontiers, holding fortified border bastions, mountain corridors, and maritime approaches, or deploying externally as Shieldhold’s national contribution to joint Lumerian coalition wars.`  
* **`Examples:`** *`I Corps ("The Shield Wall"), II Corps ("Iron Vanguard"), IV Corps (Mobile Maneuver).`

## **`Ⅴ. Army Level`**

### **`The 8 Armies of Lumeria`**

* **`Scale:`** `100,000–200,000+ Personnel per Army (2–4 Corps + Strategic Logistical Commands).`  
* **`Naming Guide:`** `Tied directly to the name of their respective kingdom or numbered according to the integrated Octarchy.`  
* **`Command Structure:`** `To maintain a strict monopoly on violence and prevent rogue civil wars among the eight kingdoms, all eight Armies answer directly to the Premier / High Sovereign as Commander-in-Chief rather than regional monarchs.`  
* **`Strategic Role:`** `The Shieldhold Army represents the largest, most heavily fortified standing professional force within the Octarchy, serving as both the military anchor of the realm and the standard for professional doctrine across Lumeria.`  
* **`Examples:`** `The Shieldhold Army, First Army of Lumeria, The Wolfen Army.`
