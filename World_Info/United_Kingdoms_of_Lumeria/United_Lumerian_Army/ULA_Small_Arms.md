# United Lumerian Army (ULA) Small Arms Ecosystem

## **Tempest Designation Rules**

**Role Identification:**  
C = Carbine  
R = Rifle  
M = Marksman  
S = Support (Machine Gun)  
H = Heavy (HMG)  
A = Anti-Materiel  
V = Vehicle

**Size Correlation:**  
As the Tier number goes up (1→2→3), the weight, caliber, and "Tempest" intensity increase.

Lastly, the first letter of the Weapon’s Name. 

Example: Rifle (Tier) 1 - Vortex = R1-V

---

# **Tier 1**

**Primary Ammunition for Tier:** T1-C — 6.5x43mm General Purpose Ball (6.5 LICC Equivalent)

**Operating System:** High-Pressure Short-Stroke Gas Piston / Multi-Lug Robust Bolt Family

**Core Design Philosophy:** Modern Ambidextrous Bullpup Architecture (Ergonomics-First Layout) Based on the Cabbage Bullpup Design

### **Shared Core Features (Standard Across All Variants of the R1 Vortex, C1 Gale, and M1 Sirocco)**

* **Logistical Standardization:** All variants share an identical serialized lower receiver core, trigger block, long-stroke bolt carrier layout, and 25-round polymer or steel magazines.  
* **Rigid Dual-Tension Linkage:** Eliminates traditional "mushy" bullpup trigger pull by utilizing solid geometric transfer rods, delivering a crisp, tactile, AR-style break.  
* **Grip-Integrated Manual of Arms:** The index finger/thumb controls (Safety and Bolt Catch/Release) are mounted adjacent to or on the pistol grip, ensuring lightning-fast reloads without moving the primary hand.  
* **True Ambidextrous Ejection:** Employs a controlled set of brass deflectors paired with the dual ejection parts to send brass forward, so casings are cleanly cast away from the shooter’s face, enabling on-the-fly "shoulder-blind" mirror shooting during close-quarters corner clearing.

  * **The Ballistic Sweet Spot:** Capitalizes on the bullpup layout to pack a full-length, ballistically optimized barrel into a weapon shorter than a standard carbine. Maximizes the 6.5x43 powder burn to deliver over 3,038 Joules of muzzle energy.  
  * **Rugged Duty Trigger:** Factory set to a heavy-duty, drop-safe 4.5lb/2.041kg break, optimized for violent combat environments, mechanical deployment drops, and high-stress scenarios.  
  * **Sustained Recoil Control:** The upscaled carrier mass combined with a bufferless dual-spring return system effortlessly absorbs the 60,000+ PSI chamber pressure, resulting in minimal muzzle climb during fully automatic fire.

## **2. C1-G “Gale” Short Carbine**

  * **Barrel Length:** 12in/304.8mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 22in/558.8mm  
  * **Empty Weight:** 6.9lbs/3.1 kg  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**

This directs all flash, concussive energy, and acoustic shock forward and away from the operator's face.  
  * **Self-Regulating Gas Block:** Engineered to artificially lengthen the gas dwell time caused by the shorter barrel. Automatically scales gas flow to violently cycle the heavy 6.5x43 bolt carrier, ensuring 100% cycling reliability even when heavily fouled by mud or environmental debris.

  * **Two-Stage Match Trigger:** Features an internal set-screw adjustment inside the transfer linkage, tuning the trigger pull down to a crisp, 2lb/0.907kg two-stage match grade break  
  * **Monolithic Harmonic Isolation:** The upper chassis features an extended, rigid handguard that completely free-floats the heavy fluted barrel. Resting the rifle heavily on bipods or barriers and ledges will not warp the barrel or shift the shooter's point of impact.  
  * **Ergonomic Marksman Stock:** Outfitted with an integrated polymer cheek riser (click-adjustable for height) and a rubberized, multi-position adjustable buttpad to ensure long-duration, fatigue-free eye alignment with high-magnification digital targeting overlays.

## **4. S1-M “Mist” Light Machine Gun (LMG)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Squad Automatic Weapon / Assault Machine Gun (Standard Fire Team Support).  
* **Real-World Base:** FN LICC-AMG / FN Evolys.  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 16.5in/419.1mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 35in/889mm to 37.5in/952.5mm  
  * **Empty Weight:** 12.1lbs/5.5kg  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **The Lateral Feed Advantage:** Traditional machine guns utilize a top-opening feed tray cover, forcing heavy optics to be mounted either too far forward or on a hinged top cover that inevitably loses its zero over time. The "Mist" eliminates this completely by employing a horizontal lateral feed system where the belt feeds from the left side and clamps down horizontally. This leaves the entire top of the upper receiver as a single, uninterrupted monolithic rail.  
  * **Monolithic Optical Line:** Due to the horizontal feed mechanism, soldiers can mount heavy thermal optics, night-vision clips, or advanced magical target-acquisition sights in a perfect, continuous line without risking a shifting zero during reloads. The weapon cleanly feeds from ultra-portable 50- or 100-round fabric "buttpacks" attached to the left side of the receiver, keeping the overall platform balanced and agile.  
  * **Ultralight Magnesium Architecture:** To prevent operator fatigue during high-intensity urban maneuvering, the receiver is machined from an ultra-lightweight magnesium alloy, reducing empty weight to a fraction of a traditional SAW. This operates via a reliable open-bolt, short-stroke gas piston system using a true free-floating barrel wrapped inside a rigid M-LOK handguard for superb heat dissipation.

# **Tier 2**

**Primary Ammunition for Tier:** T2-C — 8.6x63mm General Purpose Ball (.338 Norma Magnum Equivalent)

## **1. S2-T "Torrent" Lightweight Medium Machine Gun (LMMG)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Lightweight Medium Machine Gun (LMMG) / Platoon-Level Support.  
* **Real-World Base:** General Dynamics LWMMG (.338 Norma Magnum).  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 24in/609.6mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 49in/1244.6mm  
  * **Empty Weight:** 22.5lbs/10.2kg  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **Short Recoil Impulse Averaging:** Firing a high-energy 8.6x63mm round generates massive, violent recoil spikes that would typically destroy a lightweight infantry firearm or break the operator's shoulder. To overcome this, the Torrent utilizes a floating internal operating group. Upon ignition, the entire barrel, gas system, and bolt recoil backward together inside the outer chassis against a heavy spring and hydraulic buffer before unlocking. This stretches the violent recoil spike into a long, smooth, easily managed push.  
  * **Standard Bipod Stability:** Thanks to its impulse-averaging mechanics, the Torrent completely eliminates the need for heavy, stationary vehicle mounts or massive tripods to stay on target. A single infantry gunner can accurately lay down devastating automatic fire from a standard, lightweight bipod while deployed prone or behind light cover.  
  * **Disintegrating Link Feeding:** The platform relies on an open-bolt, gas-operated layout fed via a disintegrating metallic link belt. It is designed to be rapidly deployed with 50-round hard boxes clamped to the gun, or fed via extended belts carried in specialized assistant-gunner packs for prolonged defensive standoffs.

## **2. V2-C "Cyclone" Vehicle Mounted Machine Gun (VMMG)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Vehicle Coaxial Mounts, Remote Weapon Stations (RWS), and Pintle Mount Emplacements.  
* **Real-World Base:** Coaxial/Vehicle variant of the General Dynamics LWMMG (.338 Norma Magnum).  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 24in/609.6mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 49in/1244.6mm (Chassis footprint configured for mount linkages)  
  * **Empty Weight:** 26.8lbs/12.1 kg (Upscaled for rigid vehicle mount reinforcing)  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **Impulse Averaging Mount Protection:** Firing the high-energy 8.6x63mm tier generates intense vibrational resonance and physical stress. The Cyclone adapts the Short Recoil Impulse Averaging system into a reinforced internal carriage. By absorbing and stretching the recoil impulse inside the weapon's outer housing, it ensures that violent firing cycles do not deform vehicle mount linkages, disrupt delicate electronic turret stabilization systems, or throw off sensitive optical feeds.  
  * **Dual-Mode Solenoid Integration:** Built specifically for armor and mechanized platforms, the weapon features an integrated dual-mode firing system. It includes an electronic solenoid trigger that interfaces directly with digital vehicle fire control computers for remote operation from within the hull, alongside a tactile manual backup spade-grip assembly for standard pintle usage if electrical systems fail.  
  * **Continuous Feed Architecture:** Optimized for heavy suppression and vehicular defensive perimeters, the Cyclone dispenses with standard infantry boxes. It feeds directly from high-capacity continuous disintegrating metallic link belts routed through automated tracking channels from large 250- or 500-round ammunition bins built into the host vehicle's hull or turret assembly.

## **2. M2-S "Squall" Precision Sniper Rifle (PSR)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Advanced Precision / Sniper Rifle (Long-Range Interdiction).  
* **Real-World Base:** Barrett MRAD (Mk 22 ASR) / Accuracy International AXSR.  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 24.5in/622.3mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 38in/965.2mm to 47.5in/1206.5mm  
  * **Empty Weight:** 14.2lbs/6.4kg  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **Monolithic 20 MOA Top Rail:** Engineered for extreme-distance engagements, the Phantom features a rugged, monolithic upper receiver with an integrated full-length 20 MOA top rail. This built-in downward cant maximizes the elevation adjustment range on precision day/night optics, allowing marksmen to fully exploit the flat, wind-defying trajectory of the 8.6x63mm  
  * **Pinch-Bolt Interchangeable Barrel System:** To ensure relentless mission adaptability and streamlined maintenance, the rifle incorporates a user-interchangeable barrel system. The barrel is secured tightly to the upper receiver frame by a series of heavy-duty pinch-bolts, enabling armorers or field snipers to swap barrels rapidly without altering the receiver’s core mechanical zero.  
  * **Three-Lug 60-Degree Bolt Throw:** The manual bolt-action mechanism utilizes a highly robust, three-lug locking head that provides an incredibly tight lockup capable of containing immense chamber pressures. The bolt operates on a short, ultra-smooth 60-degree throw, granting maximum clearance for massive night-vision overlays and accelerating cycling speeds for fast follow-up shots.

# **Tier 3**

**Primary Ammunition for Tier:** T3-C — 13x110mm General Purpose Ball (Slightly larger than .50 Rus)

## **1. A3-A "Avalance" Anti Material Rifle (AMR)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Anti-Material Rifle (AMR) / Hardened Target Neutralization.  
* **Real-World Base:** Barrett M95 / DSR-50.  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 28in/711.2mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 39.5in/1003.3mm  
  * **Empty Weight:** 24lbs/10.9kg  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **Compact Bullpup Architecture:** The massive 13x110mm round requires a substantial 27-to-29in/685.8mm-to-736.6mm barrel to fully burn its heavy powder charge and reach maximum velocity. A traditional rifle layout would be unmanageably long, heavy, and front-heavy in the field. By placing the bolt action and the 5-round box magazine entirely behind the pistol grip (Bullpup), the total length of the weapon is shortened by nearly a foot without sacrificing barrel length or terminal ballistics.  
  * **Manual Bolt-Action Precision:** A manual bolt action was intentionally chosen over a semi-automatic operating system. Semi-automatic designs for a cartridge of this scale add immense mechanical weight, require complex gas routing tubes, and demand a massive reciprocating bolt carrier group that violently disrupts barrel harmonics and decreases precision. The bolt action keeps the platform mechanically rigid, reliable, and perfectly precise.  
  * **Hydro-Pneumatic Blast Mitigation:** Managing the extreme pressures of the 13mm requires a multi-stage defense system. The muzzle is capped with a tank-style dual-baffle muzzle brake that violently diverts over 50% of the expanding blast gases backward, physically pulling the rifle forward during the firing cycle. Residual energy passing into the stock is absorbed by a heavy dual-spring hydro-pneumatic shock absorber buttplate pad, safeguarding the shooter’s shoulder.

## **2. H3-Q "Quake" Heavy Machine Gun (HMG)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Heavy Machine Gun (HMG) / Static & Mobile Heavy Fire Support.  
* **Real-World Base:** Russian Kord HMG (12.7x108mm) / M2 Browning QCB.  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 38in/965.2mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 62in/1574.8mm  
  * **Empty Weight:** 56.5lbs/25.6kg  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **Fixed-Barrel Long-Stroke Gas System:** Traditional heavy machine guns (like the legacy M2 Browning) rely on short-recoil operation, meaning the massive barrel slams violently back and forth during fire, demanding heavy vehicle turrets or massive tripods to maintain accuracy. The Quake replaces this with an advanced long-stroke gas piston system located entirely underneath a structurally rigid, fixed barrel, drastically reducing erratic barrel movement.  
  * **Low-Impulse Counter-Weight Structure:** The interior mechanics are flawlessly balanced by utilizing an internal muzzle gas-rebound system paired with a low-impulse counter-weight mechanism. This counter-weight moves in perfect opposition to the heavy bolt carrier group, actively canceling out the forward and backward slamming forces of the cycle before they can pass into the frame.  
  * **Bipod-Deployable Mobility:** Because the internal recoil forces cancel each other out so efficiently, the Quake avoids the crushing weight and mounting restrictions of historical HMGs. It can be effectively and accurately fired from a lightweight infantry bipod or a light field mount, granting heavy squads unparalleled mobile anti-armor and shield-shattering firepower on foot.

## **3. V3-T "Tremor" Vehicle Heavy Machine Gun (VHMG)**

* **Role/Issuance:** Commander's Cupola Mounts, Heavy Remote Weapon Stations (RWS), and Armored Vehicle Coaxial Mounts.  
* **Real-World Base:** Russian Kord HMG (Vehicle/Coaxial variant) / M2 Browning (M48/XM705 Vehicle Mounts).  
* **Technical Specifications:**  
  * **Barrel Length:** 38in/965.2mm  
  * **Overall Length:** 62in/1574.8mm (Chassis footprint streamlined for automated armor enclaves)  
  * **Empty Weight:** 68.2lbs/31.0kg (Upscaled with a heavy-duty cooling sleeve and rigid mount housing)  
* **Special Features & Combat Optimizations:**  
  * **Stabilization Synergy & Fluid Tracking:** While the infantry variant utilizes a counter-weight to protect a foot-soldier's stance, the vehicle-bound counterpart pairs its balanced internal muzzle gas-rebound system directly with digital gyroscopic stabilizers. By mitigating over 80% of the horizontal and vertical physical vibration before it reaches the weapon frame, the gun permits vehicle targeting computers to track targets effortlessly over rough terrain without throwing off active micro-script calibration.  
  * **Integrated Solenoid & Remote Control Integration:** Purpose-built to function behind armored plating or atop unmanned turrets, the weapon strips away the manual infantry trigger pack. It is equipped with an integrated electrical solenoid system linked directly into the host platform's target acquisition software, allowing commanders or gunners to cycle payloads, manage firing rates, and trigger high-velocity suppressive sweeps remotely via control yokes.  
  * **Extended Cyclic Thermal Management:** Operating from fixed vehicle stations exposes the weapon to longer sustained suppressive windows than standard crew-served teams can manage. To counteract rapid barrel warping and thermal degradation, the barrel is enveloped in a structural heat-sink shroud engineered with heavy-duty cooling vents and built-in heat dissipation channels, preventing the weapon from overheating during continuous anti-armor or anti-ward defensive operations.
